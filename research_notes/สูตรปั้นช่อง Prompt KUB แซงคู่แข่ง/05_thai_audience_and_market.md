Research cut-off: 31 Oct 2026. Scope: Thai audience, Thai trends, Thai creator ecosystem (competitors, algorithms and clip craft are covered by other researchers). Every number is labelled with its source and date. Where only global data exists, this is stated. Secondary/aggregator sources are flagged. Pages that returned 403/402/JS-only are noted so the writer knows which figures rest on search-snippet evidence rather than a fetched page.

Addendum from coordinator (31 Oct 2026) is answered in sections 8–10 (survival/what-if resonance; Instagram Reels as a Thai discovery engine; Thai reaction to the AI-animation wave). ## 1. Platform usage in Thailand 2025–2026 and which platform drives discovery for short animated stories among Thai 18–45

### Takeaway
TikTok is effectively saturated among Thai adults (ad reach = 96.3% of 18+), Facebook is the broadest all-ages platform (51.5M), YouTube is where storytelling/animation tops the national year-end charts, but Instagram is the fastest-growing major platform (+13.9% YoY to 20.6M ad reach; 23.2M by Sept 2026) and is overwhelmingly 18–34 (67% of Thai IG users), which is exactly the age band Prompt KUB already converts. No single Thai dataset isolates "discovery of animated stories"; the evidence supports a TikTok+Instagram dual-primary strategy with YouTube Shorts/long-form as the third leg and Facebook Reels for 35–45 reach.

### Inferences
- For a 60–70 s narrated animation aimed at 18–45: TikTok gives the largest adult reach but is already at saturation and dipped −4.2% Jul–Oct 2025 (volatile ad-reach), Instagram is the growth platform and skews hardest to 18–34 (67% of IG users), and 94% IG/FB overlap means a Facebook Reels cross-post reaches the 35–45 cohort that IG under-serves (only ~14% of IG users are 45+).
- Prompt KUB's IG-first result (199K followers in ~32 days, owner-claimed) is consistent with Meta's own June 2026 messaging that single Reels in a niche can reach 2–3M views; the risk is that IG is one-third the size of TikTok/Facebook in Thailand, so a TikTok mirror is the obvious untapped reach.
- The "สั้น เร็ว ติดยาว" micro-drama habit (+56% YoY on AIS) means Thai viewers are already trained to accept serialized 1–5 minute vertical fiction with cliffhangers — the same grammar as "ตอนต่อไป".

## 2. Story genres and emotional tones that dominate Thai short-form in 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Thailand's year-end charts show that scary/ghost storytelling, adventure-survival challenges, satirical animation and edutainment are mainstream hits; TikTok Thailand's own awards created a "Storyteller of the Year" category won by a solo narrator who acts out intriguing situations; and in 2026 the AI "fruit drama" wave (soap-opera plots with betrayal and cliffhangers) proved Thai feeds will embrace AI characters. Emotional tones that travel: suspense/fear, curiosity ("รู้จาก TikTok"), moral lesson ("สอนให้รู้ว่า"), warmth/family, and Isan-flavoured humour.

### Inferences

## 3. Thai creator case studies: fast growth and the tactics they credit

### Takeaway
Thai press does not document a story/animation channel going 0→1M in 6–12 months with a timeline; the best Thai-sourced tactics come from TikTok Awards Thailand 2025 winners (speed-to-trend, first-second hooks, retention thresholds of 50–80%, dissecting your best 10 clips, "blue-ocean" angles) plus the global AI benchmark of Fruit Love Island (3.1M followers in 9 days). Prompt KUB's own owner-claimed curve (0→199K in ~32 days) is already faster than anything documented for a Thai story channel.

### Cited Findings
- bowkanyaratp (Creator of the Year 2025): channel began during COVID; viewers wanted a real "family community", not just food; **speed** — whoever films, edits and posts a trending topic first takes most views, viewers who already saw it elsewhere won't rewatch; **hook from the first second**; **retention rule** — "Data โกหกไม่ได้": clips with retention above ~50–80% get pushed, lower ones lose reach; she analyses second-by-second drop-off and cuts/retells; weak clips are dropped, good stories retold in a new way — [The Standard Wealth, 25 Nov 2025](https://thestandard.co/tiktok-insights-from-3-creators/)
- huafulldooball (Sports Creator of the Year): first clip (a rant after Liverpool lost 2–0) hit ~40,000 views four years earlier; avoids "red ocean" news for odd angles, historical stories and humour (e.g., "ปลาเตะบอล"); starting method — **post ~10 clips, break down the best performer (which words, how it opened with a question, what hooked), repeat those elements in your own style**; success = a 2-minute clip that makes someone smile — [The Standard Wealth, 25 Nov 2025](https://thestandard.co/tiktok-insights-from-3-creators/)
- paratt_ (Food Creator of the Year): formally trained chef who chose simplified "Asian Twist" content; persistence through algorithm changes; more creators = more ad money for everyone — [The Standard Wealth, 25 Nov 2025](https://thestandard.co/tiktok-insights-from-3-creators/)
- brightekkarat won Storyteller of the Year with solo narration of intriguing situations in a novel style; top clip 30M+ views — [Thansettakij, 10 Nov 2025](https://www.thansettakij.com/technology/643651)
- Global AI-animation benchmark: Fruit Love Island, created March 2026, 3.1M followers in 9 days, ~15M views/clip — [Daily Dot](https://lede-v2.dailydot.com/ai-slop-videos-of-cheating-fruit-are-taking-over-tiktok)
- Prompt KUB owner claims (ygnos.com, read 31 Oct 2026): one Instagram clip reached 1.5M views; median 437,069 accounts reached per clip; main episodes free on Instagram; flagship adapted "with permission" from @stickocalypse_shorts — [ygnos.com](https://ygnos.com/)
- Tellscore CEO Suvita Charanwong (3–6 Oct 2025): Thai influencer market grows 15–20%/yr despite the slow economy; sustaining full-time creator income is hard; advice to small creators — clear identity, a trusting community rather than raw follower count, "Human Touch", disclose AI avatars as your own representative, never make AI imagery of disasters/sensitive events — [InfoQuest Media Talk, 6 Oct 2025](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=534784)
- Thailand may have ~9M content creators from nano to mega, full- and part-time (Tellscore) — [Prachachat](https://www.prachachat.net/ict/news-1644926)
- RAiNMaker is organising a Thai Creator Association targeted for Q1 2026; iCreator Conference 2025 (26 Nov 2025, BITEC) had 4 stages, 50+ speakers, 24+ sessions — [Marketing Oops!, 27 Nov 2025](https://www.marketingoops.com/?p=354976)
- Creative Talk Conference 2025 panel "อินฟลูฯ เฟ้อ ครีเอเตอร์เอาไงต่อ: Creator Trends 2026": influencer glut, AI shaking content, short clips dominate, personal branding as survival — [Brand Buffet, Jul 2025](https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/2025/07/creator-trends-2026/)

### Inferences
- The Thai winners' playbook maps directly onto a serialized animation channel: publish a batch, identify the top 10% by retention, extract the opening question/phrase pattern, and re-template it — the same loop Prompt KUB can run on its own 437K-median Reels.
- "Speed to trend" is the tactic Thai creators stress most; for an animation channel that means a same-day "what-if" episode when a flood/earthquake/outbreak story breaks (see §8).

### Gaps
- No interview, podcast or news item was found for หลอนไดอารี่, THE GHOST RADIO, PASULOL or brightekkarat that gives follower-growth timelines or production tactics.
- No Thai AI-animation or AI-narration channel with press-documented growth figures was found (searches in Thai and English for "AI การ์ตูน", "นิทาน AI", "AI เล่าเรื่อง" returned only tool-vendor pages and fact-checks).

## 4. Thai-language and cultural conventions: hooks, tone, dialect, TTS, subtitles, sensitivities

### Takeaway
Thai-specific evidence on hook phrasing is thin but consistent: the first 3 seconds must state the premise even on mute; Thai viewers respond to vivid, extreme, decisive wording (TikTok's food-review language study: "อร่อยตะโกน"), and winners open with a question. Ghosts/superstition are mainstream, not taboo. The real red lines are the monarchy (Section 112 plus a July 2026 DE-ministry notice widening "ขัดศีลธรรมอันดี" content to cover royal family members), AI imagery of real disasters/people (Thai PBS Verify, Tellscore), and an incoming draft AI Act with transparency duties for generative content. No Thai survey on TTS-voice acceptance or muted viewing exists.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok-cited language study (Thairath): what makes Thais stop on food clips is the wording, not just visuals; three tactics — vivid/picturable vocabulary, extreme evaluation, decisive statements; example phrase "อร่อยตะโกน" — [Thairath Life (page 403; from search snippet)](https://www.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/life/2948442)
- Thai-language guide: "3 วินาทีแรกไม่ใช่ช่วงวอร์มอัป แต่มันคือรอบออดิชัน"; the first frame must make the topic clear even with sound off — [Vivideo TH](https://vivideo.ai/th/blog/hook-phuchom-video-san-yangrai)
- TikTok for Business guidance: introduce the content proposition in the first 3 seconds — [Teleprompter.com citing TikTok](https://teleprompter.com/blog/tiktok-3-second-rule)
- Thai award winners: "capture viewers from the first second" (bowkanyaratp); analyse how the best clip "opened with a question" (huafulldooball) — [The Standard Wealth, 25 Nov 2025](https://thestandard.co/tiktok-insights-from-3-creators/)
- Isan/regional flavour is chart-proven in Thai music (2025: #1 rap × luk thung; #4, #5 Isan indie) — [YouTube Thailand 2025 lists](https://blog.google/intl/th-th/products/consumer-products/youtube-eoy-trending-lists-2025/)
- Ghost storytelling is celebrated mainstream content (THE GHOST RADIO #3 creator of 2025; หลอนไดอารี่ #9) — [YouTube Thailand 2025 lists](https://blog.google/intl/th-th/products/consumer-products/youtube-eoy-trending-lists-2025/)
- Thai TTS supply exists at scale (one vendor offers 88 Thai voices with all 5 tones for TikTok-style clips) but no audience-acceptance data is published — [SpeechGen TH](https://speechgen.io/th/tts-thai/); a Thai TikTok text-to-speech how-to (2026) argues TTS gives a tight constant pace — [AnySpeech TH](https://anyspeech.io/th/blog/tiktok-text-to-speech)
- Monarchy risk: a July 2026 Ministry of Digital Economy notice widens the definition of online content "ขัดศีลธรรมอันดี" beyond Section 112 to include members of the royal family — [iLaw](https://www.ilaw.or.th/articles/58837); creators have been accused under s.112 together with Computer Crime Act s.14 (e.g., complaint against นารา เครปกะเทย) — [Sanook](https://www.sanook.com/news/1992918/); a court acquitted creator หนูรัตน์ over the "5.5" ad campaign clip for not meeting the elements of s.112 — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/crime/2822756)
- AI-image sensitivities: Tellscore's CEO says creators should not make AI imagery of sensitive events or disasters because it causes panic and fake news, and Thailand lacks strict AI oversight — [InfoQuest, 6 Oct 2025](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=534784); Thai PBS Verify documented AI deepfakes of Hun Sen "patting" a Thai deputy PM and deepfakes of presenter สรยุทธ and doctors used in diabetes-drug scams — [Thai PBS Verify](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/verify/article/content/2581)
- Draft Thai AI Act (ETDA public hearing from 9 Jul 2026, closing 14 Aug 2026): risk-tiered; a category of AI systems with transparency duties covering deepfakes, chatbots and generative AI; strict and joint liability; power to suspend/recall/block AI systems; covers developers, providers, deployers and platform operators including foreign entities — [Baker McKenzie, Jul 2026](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/07/thailand-draft-ai-act--seven-key-implications); transparency obligations for AI-generated content — [HLC](https://hlc.com/en/publications/thailand-moves-towards-comprehensive-ai-regulation-with-new-draft-ai-act)
- Thai hashtag conventions on TikTok favour lesson/knowledge framing: #สอนให้รู้ว่า (46.55M posts), #รู้จากtiktok (4.2M), #TikTokUni (40.4M) — [Brand Inside, 24 Oct 2025](https://brandinside.asia/?p=234622)

### Inferences
- Caption first lines that combine a question hook with the lesson frame ("ถ้าน้ำท่วมคอนโดคุณคืนนี้ จะเอาอะไรขึ้นไปก่อน?" / "สอนให้รู้ว่า…") align with both the winners' "open with a question" habit and the dominant Thai hashtags; no Thai study ranks specific stock phrases ("รู้ไหมว่า…", "ตอนจบไม่คาดคิด"), so these should be A/B tested rather than asserted.
- Because the top Thai storytelling channels are human-voiced, a natural, performed Thai voice (or a carefully tuned TTS with Thai tones and particles like นะ/ล่ะ/เลย) is lower-risk than flat TTS; central Thai narration with occasional Isan characters/humour matches chart evidence better than full-dialect narration.
- Fictional disasters are safe; recreating a *real* current disaster (e.g., a named flooded district) in AI imagery is the line Thai industry voices and fact-checkers warn about — label fiction clearly and avoid real place/person likenesses.

### Gaps
- No Thai survey on acceptance/rejection of TTS voices, on muted viewing/subtitle habits, or on dialect preference in narration content was found.
- No published list of top-performing Thai hook sentences for story content; the "อร่อยตะโกน" study is food-specific.

## 7. Thai audience view of AI-generated content: acceptance, backlash, labelling expectations

### Takeaway
Thais are heavy, confident AI users (9 in 10 use AI daily; 32% self-rate as "frontier" users) but 66% fear AI misuse such as deepfakes and misinformation, and Thai fact-checkers and industry leaders frame 2026 as the year AI fakes become convincing. Entertainment AI (fruit dramas) was embraced with "น้ำเน่า/brain-rot" eye-rolling rather than rejection; the backlash vocabulary targets deception and low-effort "AI slop", not fiction that is obviously animated. A draft AI Act will add transparency duties, so visible fiction/AI labelling is the safe default.

### Cited Findings
- SCBX "thAI Consumer AI Adoption 2026" (n=1,004 Thais aged 20–60, fieldwork Aug 2025 with Ipsos & Vitamins): >80% have used AI in some form; 13% use it to full potential; fewer than 1 in 5 understand how AI works; **66% worry AI could be misused (deepfakes, misinformation)**; 55% worry about privacy; segments "Skeptical Practitioner" 34% and "Smart Minimalist" 36% — [SCBX](https://www.scbx.com/en/scbx-exclusive/thai-consumer-ai-adoption/)
- Telenor Digital Lives Decoded 2025: more than 9 in 10 Thai internet users use AI in daily life, led by Gen Z — [Brand Buffet](https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/?p=275577)
- Microsoft Work Trend Index 2026 Thailand (4 Aug 2026): 32% of Thai AI users are advanced "Frontier Professionals" vs 16% globally (self-reported) — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/ai/microsoft-work-trend-index-thailand-2026/)
- Thai PBS Verify: in 2026 AI-generated clips will be far more realistic and may shape public opinion; AI voice cloning already used in Thai scams in 2025 (CIB quote) — [Thai PBS Verify](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/verify/en/article/content/8455); an AI "lion drags woman" clip hit 3M views before detection — [Thai PBS Verify](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/verify/en/content/14781)
- "AI slop" on YouTube (study via The Guardian/Kapwing, reported 31 Dec 2025): 278 of 15,000 top channels are AI slop with ~63B views, 221M subscribers and ~US$117M/yr (~3,704M THB); >20% of videos recommended to new users are AI slop; YouTube says generative AI can make both high- and low-quality content and enforces community guidelines; no Thai channels named — [The Standard, 31 Dec 2025](https://thestandard.co/ai-slop-junk-youtube-money/)
- Thai entertainment acceptance: AI fruit dramas "took over feeds" (Thai PBS, 30 May 2026) with celebrity fans, while PPTV (12 May 2026) relays netizens' "ละครน้ำเน่าแห่งยุคโซเชียล" tag and experts' "AI Brain Rot"/dopamine warnings — [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/program/ArtandCultureThaiPBS/watch/g3pbbkph5uj8); [PPTV HD36](https://www.pptvhd36.com/news/ไอที/275316)
- Tellscore's CEO: audiences trust human sincerity, so human reviewers keep growing while AI creators may take over 24/7 live commerce; creators using AI avatars should tell followers the avatar is their own representative — [InfoQuest, 6 Oct 2025](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=534784)
- Google Thailand's stated stance: AI should enhance creativity, not replace the human role — [Google Thailand blog archive](https://thailand.googleblog.com/2024_02_07_archive.html)
- Draft Thai AI Act (hearing 9 Jul–14 Aug 2026) imposes transparency duties on deepfake/chatbot/generative systems and joint liability — [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/07/thailand-draft-ai-act--seven-key-implications)

### Inferences
- An obviously animated, fictional AI series is on the accepted side of the Thai line; the two things that trigger Thai backlash are realism-based deception and volume-farmed low-effort content. Clear fiction framing, a consistent recognisable art style and a visible "สร้างด้วย AI" credit pre-empt both the fact-checker narrative and the coming transparency rules.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Thai survey specifically measures attitudes toward AI-generated entertainment clips or labelling expectations; the SCBX deepfake-concern figure is the closest proxy.

## 8. Addendum (1): Why survival / what-if scenarios (ถ้าซอมบี้ระบาด, น้ำท่วม, ติดอยู่บนดอย) resonate with Thai audiences in late 2026

## 9. Addendum (2): Has Instagram Reels become a real discovery engine for Thai 18–35 in 2025–2026 versus TikTok/Facebook?

### Takeaway
Yes for the 18–34 band, with caveats. Instagram was Thailand's fastest-growing major platform in 2025 (+13.9% YoY ad reach to 20.6M; 23.2M by Sept 2026), two-thirds of its Thai users are 18–34, Meta says it reaches up to 80% of Thai Gen Z and that Reels dominate both in-app time (>50%, global) and chat sharing (85% of shared content), and Meta chose Thailand for its first local Reels-creation campaign in April 2026. ### Cited Findings
- Instagram ad reach Thailand 20.6M (34.4% of adults 18+), +2.5M / +13.9% Oct 2024→Oct 2025 — the largest YoY gain among Facebook (+4.7%), TikTok (+5.6%), YouTube (−0.8%) and X (−1.7%) — [DataReportal, Digital 2026: Thailand](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-thailand)
