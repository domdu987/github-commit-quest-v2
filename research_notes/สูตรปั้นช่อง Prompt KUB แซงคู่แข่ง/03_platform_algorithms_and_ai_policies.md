Reading guide for the report writer

- [SECONDARY] = trade press (TechCrunch, Social Media Today, Tubefilter) or vendor blog reporting on an official statement. [UNVERIFIED] = a number or claim circulating without any primary source; do not turn these into rules.

## KQ1. Instagram Reels — ranking signals, non-follower recommendations, Trial Reels, originality crackdown, captions/hashtags/SEO, posting frequency, views counting, 2026 AI updates

### Takeaway
Instagram's only published ranking guidance remains Mosseri's "per reach" framing: watch time, likes-per-reach and sends-per-reach are the top three signals (Jan 2025), re-stated in Oct 2026 with sends-per-reach singled out as the signal for reaching non-followers, and comments explicitly downgraded. Distribution to non-followers is a staged "small likely-to-enjoy audience → wider → wider" test that is officially independent of follower count (announced Apr 2024, fully launched Feb 2025). Since then Instagram has layered on eligibility gates rather than new signals: aggregator/repost accounts lose recommendations (Reels 2024; photos and carousels 30 Apr 2026), unlabeled AI-person profiles lose recommendations (31 Aug 2026), hashtags are capped at five (18 Dec 2025), and Mosseri says AI content is not throttled "just for being AI" but must be labeled.

### Cited Findings

A. Ranking signals (official statements, dated)
- 22 Jan 2025, Mosseri ranking video series: "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends"; creators should track "average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach"; "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." Comments were not in the list. [OFFICIAL quote via tracker] — [Keywords Everywhere Instagram update tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/); [ZoomSphere summary of Mosseri statements](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach)
- 25–26 Feb 2025, Mosseri: Instagram computes both relative watch time (completion rate) and absolute watch time (seconds watched); he said the app does not penalize longer posts (a 10-second and a 60-second video are treated the same in that respect); weights were not disclosed. [SECONDARY reporting an official statement] — [Social Media Today, 25 Feb 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-longer-video-watch-time-versus-completion-rate/740916/); [Digital Information World, 26 Feb 2025](https://www.digitalinformationworld.com/2025/02/instagram-provides-more-insights-on.html)
- 26 Feb 2025, Instagram for Creators ("Helping creators of all sizes break through"): ranking uses (1) predicted interest from user activity — watch time, retention, shares, likes, comments — and (2) "audience matching" (what people with similar interests engage with). Advice: hook viewers in the first 3 seconds; track "View Rate" in Insights (share of viewers who keep watching past 3 seconds); add audio to reels, photos and carousels; "post regularly." [OFFICIAL] — [Instagram for Creators, 26 Feb 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through)
- May 2023 (background, still Instagram's standing help text): recommendations consider "how many people like, comment, share, and save a post, and how quickly they do so," plus the viewer's history with the poster; use Accounts Reached to see how much reach comes from non-followers. [OFFICIAL] — [Instagram for Creators, "Tips for Improving Your Reach", 31 May 2023](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/tips-for-improving-your-reach)
- Sept 2026, Mosseri's 3-part "how ranking works" video series (3 Sept, 8 Sept, 17 Sept 2026): part two's caption names engagement, originality, recency, "breaking content" and "larger AI models" as the things that change the systems; no weights or ranked order are given. [OFFICIAL posts, summarized by a secondary guide] — [kabo.sh guide with links to the three Instagram posts](https://kabo.sh/guides/instagram-ranking-2026); posts: [Part 1](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc1CcoYgZD_/), [Part 2](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdENi2fP59I/), [Part 3](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdbYqrtPDzd/)
- Oct 2026, Mosseri on the Colin & Samir show (shared as a collab reel; reported as 23 Oct 2026, kabo.sh dates the reel 28 Oct 2026): "Sends per reach is particularly important for that unconnected reach"; metrics for a new creator in order: "Sends per reach, likes per reach, and then watch time or average watch time"; "We focus way less on watch time than all the competition does, but we do care about watch time"; "People think we rank just to drive engagement, but we actually rank for a lot of other things" (originality, recency, giving new content a chance to break out); "We try to give every piece of content a chance... a brand new account can get millions of views, which was impossible two years ago." [OFFICIAL quotes via secondary] — [ZoomSphere](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach); [kabo.sh](https://kabo.sh/guides/instagram-ranking-2026) (reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfAik8-piUm/); [BuzzInContent (search excerpt; page returned 403 on fetch)](https://www.buzzincontent.com/news/instagram-weighs-watch-time-less-than-rivals-shares-key-to-reaching-non-followers-mosseri-12759750)
- 23 Oct 2026, Mosseri Stories Q&A: comments are "not the most important part of value by a long shot"; "We actually focus a lot more on finding reels and photos that you might send to a friend"; "more and more of the reach on Instagram came from recommendations." [OFFICIAL via secondary] — [ZoomSphere](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach)
- Background (May 2024 AMA): asked whether watch time matters more than likes or comments, Mosseri said "send rates are actually more important for reach"; Instagram does not offer a native sends-per-reach metric (creators divide Sends by Reach themselves). [SECONDARY] — [Lindsey Gamble timeline](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/unpacking-instagrams-focus-on-sends-per-reach-a-timeline-why-it-matters-and-tips-for-creators-brands)

B. How Reels reach non-followers (test distribution)
- 30 Apr 2024 announcement: previously reels in recommendation surfaces were ranked mainly by how the account's followers engaged; the new approach shows each eligible piece of content "to a small audience that it thinks will enjoy it," whether or not they follow the creator, and the top performers go to "a slightly wider audience," then wider again. Eligible = original, no visible watermarks, meets Recommendation Guidelines. [OFFICIAL] — [Instagram for Creators, "Helping Creators Find New Audiences", 30 Apr 2024](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality); [TechCrunch, 30 Apr 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators)
- 26 Feb 2025: Instagram said this system is now fully launched; new reels are shown to followers and non-followers; among non-followers a reel first goes to a small audience expected to enjoy it, and distribution is based on engagement "not just the creator's follower count." Example cited: @pov_husband, created April 2024, >1.2 billion reel views and 0 → 2 million followers in under a year. [OFFICIAL] — [Instagram for Creators, 26 Feb 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through)
- Length ceiling for recommendations: "In order to be recommended, they must be 3 minutes or less" (help text written when the cap was 90 s). [OFFICIAL] — [Tips for Improving Your Reach](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/tips-for-improving-your-reach). The Reels upload cap was raised from 90 s to 3 minutes on 20 Jan 2025; Instagram previously did not recommend reels over 90 s and now does. [SECONDARY] — [Social Media Today, Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-officially-expands-reels-length-to-3-minutes/737766/); [Social Media Today (Instagram creator event)](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-reels-tips-3-minutes-unfiltered-nyc/741722/); [Lindsey Gamble](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/instagram-three-minute-reels)
- Recommendation Guidelines: content is allowed but not recommended if it is e.g. "low-quality repurposed content," clickbait/engagement bait, sexually suggestive, political, regulated goods, misinformation; repeat offenders become ineligible for recommendations for a period; Account Status shows eligibility and allows deletion/appeal. [OFFICIAL] — [Instagram for Creators, recommendations and originality](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)

C. Trial Reels
- Launched 10 Dec 2024 (rolled out to selected creators that week, "globally in the coming weeks"): a "Trial" toggle in the Reels posting flow shows the reel first to users who don't follow you; it sits in drafts, not on the grid or Reels tab; only the creator sees it is a trial; after ~24 hours you see views, likes, comments, shares vs previous trials; followers may still see it if someone shares it; you can "share with everyone," or set auto-share so Instagram shares it with followers "if it performs well based on views in the first 72 hours." [OFFICIAL via SMT] — [Social Media Today, 10 Dec 2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-launches-trial-reels-for-audience-sampling/735136/); [9to5Mac, 10 Dec 2024](https://9to5mac.com/2024/12/10/instagrams-new-trial-reels-feature-lets-creators-test-content-with-non-followers)
- Instagram's claimed test stats (40% of trial users post more often; 80% see more non-follower reach) circulate only secondhand through vendor blogs. [UNVERIFIED] — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/are-instagram-trial-reels-worth-it)
- 2026: Trial reels can be scheduled in-app; sharing setting Off / Manual / Auto (auto = shared to followers if it does well in its first 72 hours); up to 20 trial reels per day per Instagram FAQ (as relayed). [SECONDARY/vendor] — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/can-you-schedule-trial-reels); [Superprofile changelog, Oct 2026](https://superprofile.bio/changelog/instagram-trial-reels-scheduler-october-2026); [Kapwing](https://kapwing.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels-study-what-we-learned-after-a-trial-reel-hit-1-million-views)
- 16 Oct 2026, Mosseri: trials are for "if you're just trying to experiment with lots of different reels"; re-posting the same trial reel repeatedly "is not okay. We're going to crack down on that." 30 Oct 2026: posting several *different* trial reels in a day is "totally fine." [OFFICIAL via secondary] — [ZoomSphere](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach)
- Kapwing's small test (7 Oct 2025; two reel pairs; 5-day measurement): a trend-based reel got 2,258 views as a trial vs 949 as a regular reel (2.3x); a product reel got 802 vs 836; trials gained 1 follower total; its unrelated 1.4M-view viral reel produced 130 new followers, 8.6K profile visits, 290 link taps. Trials cannot be boosted as ads. D. Originality / aggregator crackdown (dates and exact rules)
- 30 Apr 2024 (Reels): when two or more identical pieces are detected, only the original is recommended and copies are replaced in Explore, Reels and in-feed recommendations (when the original is relatively new and the audio/visual match is confident); content that is "materially changed" (meme, parody compilation, new voiceover, reaction remix) is not replaced; copies get a label linking to the original creator; accounts that "repeatedly post others' content without creating or materially enhancing it" become ineligible for recommendations and can regain eligibility after enough time passes since the last unoriginal post (check in Account Status, appeal available); "publishers with licensing agreements or explicit permissions are excluded"; followers still see aggregator posts. [OFFICIAL] — [Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality); [TechCrunch, 30 Apr 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators)
- The commonly quoted "10 or more reposts in the last 30 days" threshold appears in secondary coverage of the 2024 rule, not on Instagram's own page. [SECONDARY] — [Gigazine, 2 May 2024](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240502-instagram-updated-algorithm-prioritizes-original-content); referenced again in [Social Media Today, 30 Apr 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-algorithm-to-benefit-original-creators/819016/)
- 30 Apr 2026 (expansion to photos and carousels): accounts that "regularly repost content they didn't create, or primarily share other people's work as photos and carousels" are no longer eligible for recommendations anywhere in the app (feeds, Explore/"Discover"); reviewed on a rolling 30-day basis — an account whose posts in a 30-day window are "mostly re-posts" is an aggregator; original = content wholly created, reflecting a unique perspective, or "materially edited"; meme templates/popular clips are fine only with "an element that enhances the content" ("an original meme transforms another creator's photo or video... This is the kind of creativity we want to continue rewarding"); NOT original: low-effort edits such as adding a watermark or changing speed, and screenshots of someone's post even with their username for credit; test for third-party material = edits must "add real value beyond just simply restating or referencing the third-party content"; exemption for "a set of publishers we've identified who have licensing agreements and/or explicit permissions"; use Collabs, Remix, paid-partnership label, Stories shares or the repost tool for attribution; followers unaffected. [OFFICIAL via TechCrunch/SMT] — [TechCrunch, 30 Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/); [Social Media Today, 30 Apr 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-algorithm-to-benefit-original-creators/819016/)
- Recovery, per the tracker: most of the last 30 days' posts must be original; standing is visible in Account Status; no numeric threshold in the 2026 policy. [SECONDARY] — [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- Note on dates: the "July 2025 crackdown on unoriginal content" was Meta's *Facebook* announcement (see KQ4); there was no separate Instagram announcement in July 2025. [SECONDARY] — [TechCrunch, 14 Jul 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/14/following-youtube-meta-announces-crackdown-on-unoriginal-facebook-content)
- Background (Feb 2021, still enforced): reels that are "visibly recycled from other apps (i.e. contains logos or watermarks)," blurry/low-resolution, mostly covered by text, or bordered are made "less discoverable" in the Reels tab; Mosseri later clarified that your *own* logo is fine. [OFFICIAL via secondary] — [Tubefilter, 10 Feb 2021](https://tubefilter.com/2021/02/10/instagram-reels-with-tiktok-watermark-less-discoverable/); [Social Media Today (Mosseri on own logo)](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/)
- 30 Oct 2026, Mosseri: "We don't demote content for being created with other apps other than ours, as long as they don't have watermarks on them." [OFFICIAL via secondary] — [ZoomSphere](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach)

### Inferences
- For a story-animation account, the per-clip optimization order Mosseri gives new creators is explicit: sends/reach first, likes/reach second, average watch time third. A clip that makes a viewer DM it to a friend ("ส่งให้เพื่อน") is the strongest lever for non-follower reach; comment-bait is officially low value.
- Because Instagram grades rates "per reach," not totals, a 2025–2026 account with few followers can out-rank an older account on the same test audience; the older account's advantage (follower engagement) only strengthens the *connected* likes signal, which Mosseri rates as slightly less important for recommendations.
- The "สวนสุดท้าย" adaptation (with permission from @stickocalypse_shorts) is safe only if each clip is "materially changed" by Instagram's definition (own animation, new Thai script/voiceover, new structure) — permission alone is not an automatic exemption; the exemption applies to "a set of publishers we've identified." Visible attribution via Collab/Remix tools, not screenshots or a copied clip with a watermark, is the documented route.
- The 31 Aug 2026 rule targets profiles "featuring an AI-generated person." A cartoon/animated channel is not described by that wording, but Instagram has not said so explicitly; labeling at the media level ("AI info" via the upload toggle/metadata) carries no stated reach penalty and matches Mosseri's "label it" stance.
- Posting cadence has no official cap; Mosseri says extra reels do not cannibalize one another, so the beneficiary can safely raise frequency (see KQ6 data) and use Trial Reels for format experiments — but must not re-upload the same trial reel.

### Gaps
- Instagram's formal "ranking explained" help text is still dated 2023; there is no official weighting of watch time vs sends vs likes, no official "good" sends-per-reach value, and no native sends-per-reach metric.
- Whether an animated/cartoon AI character channel is covered by the "AI-generated profile" rule is not addressed in the official post; no help-center definition of "AI-generated person" was found.
- The exact recovery rule for aggregator status in 2026 (share of original posts / days since last repost) is only reported by a tracker, not on an Instagram page I could open.
- Instagram's own Trial Reels statistics (40%/80%) could not be verified from a primary source.
- Replay handling in the 2025 "Views" definition is inconsistent across vendor documentation; Meta's own metric documentation was not fetched.

## KQ2. TikTok — ranking signals, For You feed testing, 2025–2026 AI-content changes (labeling, AI slider, spam detection), Creator Rewards eligibility (incl. Thailand), and trend reports on story-driven content

### Takeaway
TikTok's only official ranking description still says recommendations are driven by user interactions, video information (captions, sounds, hashtags) and device/account settings, with strong signals such as finishing a longer video weighted far above weak ones, and "neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors." ## KQ3. YouTube Shorts — ranking, view counting (2025 and 2026 changes), 3-minute Shorts, July 2025 "inauthentic content" YPP policy, AI disclosure rules (May 2026 update), and Shorts/long-form interplay

### Takeaway
YouTube describes Shorts as a feed where each video is auto-played and tested: the Shorts-feed equivalents of impressions and CTR are "Shown in feed" and "Viewed vs. swiped away," and ranking is judged per video and topic, not per channel. Public Shorts views have counted every start/replay since 31 Mar 2025 (and all formats since 24 Aug 2026), while money and YPP eligibility still run on "engaged views"/qualified views. The 15 Jul 2025 YPP change renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" to cover mass-produced, templated output (including AI slideshows with identical narration) and is enforced channel-wide; AI tools remain allowed when the result shows the creator's own creative vision. Clearly unrealistic animation needs no AI disclosure, and a disclosure label "alone does not change how a video is recommended or whether it's eligible to earn money."

## KQ4. Facebook Reels — distribution for Pages/Profiles in 2025–2026, the July 2025 unoriginal-content enforcement, Content Monetization (incl. Thailand), and Facebook's strength for Thai audiences

### Takeaway

## KQ5. Cross-platform — why later-starting accounts overtake older ones (test distribution, topic consistency, cadence, "dead audience," format pivots, watermark/cross-posting penalties) and how to relaunch a stalled account

### Takeaway
All four platforms now officially distribute new clips through follower-independent tests: Instagram's staged "small likely-to-enjoy audience → wider" ranking (2024–2025), TikTok's interest-based For You testing where "neither follower count nor... previous high-performing videos are direct factors," and YouTube's per-video, per-topic judgment ("We look at video and topic," not channel). This is why a 2025–2026 account posting more frequent, original, consistently themed clips can overtake an older account: it accumulates more test wins per week, and per-reach signals favor clips over accounts. The "dead audience" theory is not supported by any official statement — Mosseri says removing inactive followers does not meaningfully change reach — but recommendation *eligibility* penalties (reposting, watermarks, unlabeled AI persons, repeated identical trial reels, 30-day inactivity associations) are documented and do stall accounts; the documented recovery path is Account Status cleanup plus a run of original posts.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram: eligible content is shown "to a small audience that it thinks will enjoy it" regardless of follow status, then wider; distribution "based on engagement, not just the creator's follower count"; "a brand new account can get millions of views, which was impossible two years ago" (Mosseri, Oct 2026); example of @pov_husband 0 → 2M followers in <1 year from an April 2024 start. [OFFICIAL] — [Instagram for Creators, 30 Apr 2024](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality); [Instagram for Creators, 26 Feb 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through); [ZoomSphere (Mosseri quote)](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach)
- TikTok: "neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors"; the feed deliberately includes videos outside stated interests to gauge wider appeal; duplicates and already-seen content are excluded. [OFFICIAL] — [TikTok Newsroom, 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- YouTube: "We don't look at channel or creator for those sorts of things. We look at video and topic" (Rene Ritchie); Shorts and long-form are separated in watch history (Todd Sherman); Shorts views are tested per video in the feed. [OFFICIAL via secondary] — [The Leap](https://www.theleap.co/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/08/26/rene-ritchie-shorts-creator-faqs/); [TechCrunch, 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/25/youtube-demystifies-the-shorts-algorithm-views-and-answers-other-creator-questions/)
- Per-reach grading: Mosseri's signals are "likes per reach" and "sends per reach" (Jan 2025), i.e., rates among people who saw the post, not totals. [OFFICIAL via tracker] — [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- Cadence compounding: Buffer (2.1M posts, 102K accounts, Aug 2025) found median reach *per post* rises with frequency (+12% at 3–5/week, +18% at 6–9, +24% at 10+ vs 1–2/week) and weekly follower-growth rate rises monotonically (+0.12%, +0.26%, +0.44%, +0.66% vs no posting); no cannibalization observed. [SECONDARY study] — [Buffer, 13 Aug 2025](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)

[OFFICIAL via secondary] — [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/); [ZoomSphere](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach)
- "Dead audience"/ghost followers: Mosseri has said inactive/bot follower removals do not affect how many people you reach; in a July 2026 AMA he answered that cleaning up a follower list does not significantly hurt Reels reach or ranking (removal only stops those accounts seeing your content). Third-party blogs asserting that ghost followers "drag down" reach provide no platform source. [OFFICIAL via secondary; counter-claims UNVERIFIED] — [What's On (Mosseri Stories)](https://whatson.ae/?p=388415); [Kontentino, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/remove-followers-instagram-reach/); [Chalene (counter-claim example)](https://chalene.com/should-you-remove-ghost-followers-on-instagram/)
- Why follower reach is low regardless: Mosseri has explained that many followers simply do not open the app on the day you post (search excerpt; article fetch returned 403). [SECONDARY] — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/instagram-why-content-isnt-reaching-followers-439028)
- Inactivity: secondhand summaries of Mosseri say a few days off does not hurt, while ~30-day absences are associated with lower reach when resuming; Instagram has never published a required cadence. [SECONDARY] — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)
- Topic/interest matching: Instagram's second ranking input is "audience matching" (what people with similar interests engage with); TikTok recommendations are built from expressed interests; YouTube evaluates "video and topic." [OFFICIAL] — [Instagram for Creators, 26 Feb 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through); [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you); [The Leap (Ritchie)](https://www.theleap.co/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- Eligibility penalties that stall accounts (all documented above): Instagram aggregator ineligibility (Reels 2024; photos/carousels 30 Apr 2026) with recovery once posts are mostly original again; unlabeled AI-person profiles (31 Aug 2026); Facebook repeat-reuse → reduced distribution on *everything* the account shares and temporary loss of monetization (Jul 2025); TikTok FYF ineligibility for "unoriginal or reused material without anything new" (Sept 2026 guidelines); YouTube channel-level demonetization for inauthentic content (Jul 2025). — [TechCrunch 30 Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/); [Instagram for Creators 31 Aug 2026](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label); [Meta for Creators 14 Jul 2025](https://creators.facebook.com/blog/combating-unoriginal-content); [TikTok Community Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/)

- Relaunch tools that exist officially: Instagram Account Status (see what is ineligible, delete, appeal) and Trial Reels (test new formats on non-followers without touching followers' feeds; repeated identical trials will be cracked down on); Facebook Professional Dashboard post-level insights and Support home risk indicator; YouTube Studio disclosure status and channel-level monetization review. — [Instagram Tips page](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/tips-for-improving-your-reach); [SMT Trial Reels](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-launches-trial-reels-for-audience-sampling/735136/); [ZoomSphere (Mosseri on trials)](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach); [Meta for Creators](https://creators.facebook.com/blog/combating-unoriginal-content); [YouTube Help disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)
- Follower conversion is weak from pure discovery: Kapwing's 1.4M-view reel produced 130 followers; trial reels produced ~1 follower per ~3,000 views in its tiny test. [SECONDARY, directional] — [Kapwing](https://kapwing.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels-study-what-we-learned-after-a-trial-reel-hit-1-million-views)

### Inferences
- The mechanism by which later accounts overtake: (a) each clip is tested on a cold audience whose response is graded per reach, so clip quality beats account age; (b) more clips per week = more lottery tickets and, per Buffer, no per-post penalty; (c) consistent topic/style gives the matching systems a clean interest cluster so each win compounds into the next; (d) older accounts frequently carry eligibility debt (reposts, watermarks, mixed formats, long gaps) that newer accounts do not. A format pivot does not "reset the algorithm" in any documented way, but it changes the audience-matching cluster, so early tests after a pivot behave like a new account's.
- The "dead audience" effect is unproven; what is documented is that followers mostly do not see posts because they are not online, and that likes-per-reach from followers matters slightly more for connected reach — so a large, disengaged follower base is at worst a neutral factor for recommendations, not a penalty.
- A restart on the same handle is viable: clear Account Status, stop any reposting/aggregation, remove watermarked uploads, post only original material for 30+ days (the 2026 rolling-30-day review), use Trial Reels for format tests, and keep a steady 3–5+ posts/week. Starting a fresh account is only indicated when the old one has hard, unappealable eligibility restrictions.

### Gaps
- No platform has published how large the initial test audience is, how many stages exist, or how long a test runs; "72 hours" appears only as the Trial Reels auto-share window.
- No official study on "old disengaged followers dragging down distribution"; the only official statements are Mosseri's "no, not significantly" on follower removal.
- No official guidance on whether topic pivots reset interest matching; this is inferred from the stated use of audience matching.
- Cross-platform watermark detection details (how Instagram/Facebook identify TikTok or CapCut marks) are not disclosed.

## KQ6. Data on cadence and length — credible 2025–2026 studies on posting frequency vs reach, optimal Reels/Shorts/TikTok length, and follower conversion

### Takeaway
The strongest frequency evidence is Buffer's Aug 2025 study: median reach per post and follower-growth rate both rise at every frequency tier up to 10+ feed posts per week, with diminishing but still positive returns, and Instagram's leadership says extra posts do not cannibalize. On length, official rules set the envelope (Instagram recommends only reels ≤3 minutes; TikTok's rewards program requires >1 minute; Shorts ≤3 minutes) while platform leaders say longer is not penalized if watch time holds; third-party "optimal length" datasets conflict (Shorts 40–60 s or 13 s/60 s; Reels 60–90 s; TikTok 21–34 s), so the defensible rule is to optimize completion/average watch time at whatever length the story needs. Follower conversion from discovery is low and no platform publishes benchmarks.

### Inferences
- Cadence rule supported by data: move from ≤2 to at least 3–5 feed posts/week (Reels) and treat 6–9/week as the next step; there is no evidence of per-post reach loss up to 10+/week, and Instagram leadership says posts don't cannibalize — the binding constraint is production quality, not the algorithm.
- Length rule supported by official statements: build each episode to the length the story needs within 60–180 s (recommendable on IG and Shorts; >60 s satisfies TikTok CRP), front-load a 3-second hook, and judge by average watch time + completion + sends/reach rather than by a fixed duration; A/B 45–60 s vs 75–90 s cuts via Trial Reels.
- Follower conversion from recommendations is structurally low (~0.01% per view in the one dataset found), so growth plans should be denominated in views/sends and in-clip calls to follow the series, not in followers per clip.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 study isolates animated/story content or Thai-language content for cadence or length.
- Buffer's dataset pools photos, Reels and carousels; no Reels-only frequency curve exists.
- No platform publishes follower-conversion benchmarks; the Kapwing figures come from one account.
- Socialinsider's reach-by-format figures and the 2026 reach update could not be read from the primary pages; the TikTok "optimal length" attributions to TikTok itself are unverified.
