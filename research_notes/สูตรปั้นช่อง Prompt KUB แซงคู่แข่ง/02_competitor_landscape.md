## Key question 0 — Baseline: what is Prompt KUB, how fast did it actually grow, and on which platforms? (reference point for "started after us and overtook us")

### Takeaway
Prompt KUB is itself the fastest-growing Thai account found in this niche: by its own Meta-Graph-API figures it went from its first IG post (23 Sep 2026) and 8 followers (1 Oct) to 198,931 IG followers and 74.7M views from 104 posts by 25 Oct 2026, i.e. ~32 days; its YouTube channel (created Aug 2025, long dormant with AI demo clips) pivoted on 20 Oct 2026 to daily 8–60-minute Thai AI-narrated survival stories and gathered ~1.16M of its 1.41M lifetime views in 11 days. It does not use TikTok at all.

### Inferences
- Implied IG cadence: 43 posts in the first 25 days (≈1.7/day) then 61 more posts between 18 and 25 Oct (≈8.7 "posts"/day; Graph API media counts may include non-Reel posts), and ≈3.25 posts/day averaged over 23 Sep–25 Oct. Implied growth 18→25 Oct: +172.7K followers (≈24.7K/day) and +64.6M views in 7 days. These are derived from owner-claimed figures.
- 29 of 104 posts above 1M views (28%) and a 718K average view per post are far above every Thai competitor measured below; the inflection coincides with adopting the Stickocalypse template (Part 1 of the original was published 12 Oct 2026, see KQ3), i.e. Prompt KUB adapted within days of the original's release.
- Prompt KUB's platform mix is unusual among Thai peers: IG-first (Reels 60–70 s), YouTube as a long-form (8–60 min) second channel, no TikTok. Most Thai peers below are YouTube-first or TikTok-first.
- The two long-form YouTube series show the same decay pattern (EP1 ≈ 4–5x the last episode) seen in every serialized channel in this file; standalone 48–59-minute "จบในตอน" videos out-perform mid-series episodes (108K–184K vs 25K–73K).

## Key question 1 — The Thai Sept–Oct 2026 wave of Stickocalypse-style / survival-scenario AI animation: who started when, how big now, who is bigger than Prompt KUB, how formats differ

### Takeaway
At least 17 Thai channels in the "ถ้าซอมบี้ระบาด / เอาชีวิตรอด" AI-animation format were created or pivoted between 14 Sep and 28 Oct 2026; none of them matches Prompt KUB's IG follower count, but on YouTube several are growing on comparable per-video numbers (นับวันรอด: 714K views and 9.47K subs in 14 days from 8 videos; Jay Survivals: ~1M views in 11 days from 10 videos), while the bulk of new entrants (Genboy, laoreuangtoon, Stickman Ch, What If เรื่องเล่าโลกโหด, มนุษย์อัปเดต, หนังสั้นAl, SurvivalTheory101) are stuck at 15–263 subscribers because they dump whole series at once, use generic titles and do not localise. The only Thai AI-story channel clearly bigger than Prompt KUB on YouTube is Sigma Lens (672K subs, 139.6M views, 654 videos since 27 Mar 2026), a mass-production narration channel that runs survival "what-if" alongside ghost and fantasy series.

## Key question 4 — What fast growers have in common vs slow/stalled AI-animation accounts (cadence, format discipline, series hooks, native VO, platform, timing, topics)

### Takeaway
Across 40+ accounts measured, every fast grower combines (1) daily or multi-daily output on fixed clock slots, (2) one serialized universe with numbered episodes, recurring characters and an explicit "next part" pull, (3) a viewer-as-protagonist "ถ้าคุณ/If you" hook in the title and first seconds, (4) native-language AI narration with burned subtitles, (5) a cheap, consistent visual system (stick/flat/SVG or one cat family) that makes volume possible, and (6) localisation of setting; the stalled accounts share burst-dumping whole series within minutes, hashtag-only or generic titles, no recurring cast, unlocalised verbatim copies, and channel identities that mix unrelated topics.

- Spacing matters more than volume: channels posting 1–3 pieces per day on fixed slots grow; channels posting 5–11 pieces within minutes do not, even with the same premise.
