## Hook test: on a phone, in the first 3 s, is it instantly strange and readable?
Half of it is. "Calm man with an umbrella" reads from about 1.3 s, and well at 2.0 s (f_013-016). "Every coachman hates him" does not read before 3.9 s. In shot 1 the coachman is a tiny figure in the background. In shot 2 (2.2-3.9 s) one small coachman is frowning with folded arms, which reads as neutral on 360 px. The cabbage hit at about 4.0 s (f_024-028) is clear and funny. It comes after the 3 s window, and the coachman who throws it is mostly hidden behind the umbrella. A viewer at 3 s sees "a man walks in the rain past a coach". The conflict is not there yet.

## The 3-5 most valuable changes
1. **Shot 2 (coach_low): make the hostility the picture.** Use the box_trio framing (tile 10), with all three coachmen on their boxes in a row, larger in frame (heads around 60-80 px on the phone, i.e. camera about 30 % closer or a longer lens on the boxes). Key a visible head turn (profile to glare at frame start, holding about 0.7 s), add scowling brows, and have the head coachman point his whip down at Hanway. Hanway stays small in the lower left, walking past, uncropped (stop his approach before he hits the left edge; nothing past f_017).
2. **Shot 1: land Hanway readable by 0.8 s.** Start the push-in tighter, or start him 30-40 % closer, so the umbrella silhouette is at least about 40 px on the phone in the first second. Put the head coachman's glare/pointing pose on in the background coach from frame 0, so both halves of the hook are present in shot 1.
3. **Bring the first projectile inside 3 s if possible.** For example, a cabbage leaf or rotten veg from coachman_b hits the umbrella at about 2.8-3.0 s in shot 2, so the "throws rubbish" beat of the narration shows up early. Keep the big cabbage bounce in shot 3. If the timing is locked, at least keep the shot 3 thrower's face clear of the umbrella during the wind-up (move the camera about 10 deg right, or lift the coachman card) so the viewer sees who throws.
4. **Use the empty bottom band.** Tilt the camera about 5 deg up, or raise it slightly, in shots 2-3 so the coach and coachmen sit larger and lower. Keep heads above y 880 and leave the caption band as wet setts with reflections.

## Verdict: FIX-FIRST
The look is good: sharp, warm/cool, dense, no text. The key moment does not work on a phone, though. In the first 3 s the "coachmen hate him" half of the hook is not readable, and shot 2's glare (the beat meant to carry it) is unreadable at phone size. Changes 1 and 2 are the blockers. Changes 3 and 4 are strongly recommended.
