## Are the three years countable and rising?
**Yes.** There are three shots, three projectiles and three sizes. Year 1: a green cabbage arcs in and slaps the dome, with the leaf stuck (small on the phone, but the throw gesture carries it). Year 2: an egg leaves a white/yellow splat on the dome and the thrower laughs; this reads clearly. Year 3: a coach and a wave of mud cover him head to toe. Each beat is bigger than the last, and the cuts land on "ปีแรก / ปีที่สอง / ปีที่สาม".

## Is the mud splash a funny, readable splash (crown + droplets, not a sheet or blobs)? **No, not yet.**
- At the peak (f_030-032) the splash is a row of flat-shaded brown triangular fins standing on the road, plus clusters of round brown balls (the "bulb tips", and a heap of balls around the wheel hub). On the phone it reads as a brown picket fence or a row of thorns with a pile of chocolate balls. It does not read as liquid. This is exactly what the client rule forbids: flat sheets and round blobs.
- It does not rise as a crown. It stays below Hanway's chest, does not arc over him, and he is never shown being "hit head to toe". The result (mud spots on his face, coat and legs) tells it after the fact.
- It does not come from the puddle the camera is looking across. The foreground puddle (puddle_low) stays glassy and calm while the splash happens behind it. The wave has no source.
- Hanway himself is readable: shocked face, umbrella flung up, then the deadpan mud face. The after-gag works.

## Most valuable changes
1. **Rebuild the splash shape (blocker).** Make a curved spray crown that rises from the wheel/puddle contact and arcs toward and over Hanway. Use thin, tapered, slightly translucent sheets that break into streaks at the rim, then many small teardrop/streak droplets (elongated along the motion, varied size, most well under 15 px, none perfectly round), peaking above his hat at about g795 and falling on him by g800. Shade with two or three tones of brown, with a light rim catching the window amber, so it reads as wet mud and not as flat card. Remove the ball clusters at the hub and the bulb tips on the fins. The crown in tile 23 has the right silhouette height; only its blobs were wrong.
2. **Give the splash a source in frame.** Have the wheel cut through the foreground puddle, or move the camera so the puddle the coach hits is the one in front of the lens. Add a V-wake/sheet of water torn up at the wheel and ripples racing across the foreground puddle for g786-800. The empty bottom 40 % then pays off.
3. **Make Hanway bigger in shot 12.** Move the camera about 25 % closer to him, or shift him from the left third toward x ≈ 0.35 W, so his face (shocked, then deadpan) is at least about 45 px on the phone. The deadpan hold (g800-866) is the laugh, and it needs to be legible.
4. (Polish) Year 1: let the cabbage leaf on the dome be larger, or a brighter green with a dark outline, so "hit" still reads on the phone after the throw.
