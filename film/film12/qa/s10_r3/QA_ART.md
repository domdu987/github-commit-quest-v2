## Remaining changes (shot 12, g796-810 only)
1. **Replace the oval landing blobs (blocker).** In g798-806, anything in the air must be a pointed streak (stretched along its fall direction, tapered at both ends), like the shards above. Mud that lands should appear only as opaque splat dots or drips on his body, with the same stuck-mud shapes he keeps for the hold. Remove the soft translucent ellipses in front of him and beside him.
2. **Make the ripples water, not wire.** Use fewer rings (2-3 per impact, not 7 overlapping), make them much smaller in the near puddle, and lower contrast: a dark-and-light pair or a faint highlight at ~30-40 % opacity, tinted to the sky reflection, not pure white. Thin and fade each ring as it expands, and anti-alias them. They should be read as a disturbance in the reflection, not as drawn lines. If time is short, cut the near-puddle card back to 1-2 soft rings plus the small droplets. That is better than the current rings.
