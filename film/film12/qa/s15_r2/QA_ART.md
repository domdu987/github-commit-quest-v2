## Reveal test: is the mechanism ONE clear picture on a phone?
**Yes, now mostly.** On the phone sheet, shot 16 (g1118-1193) reads in one look: heavy rain, a pack of angry people shoving at the coach door, passengers already crammed on, and the coachman perched above them with his purse. The rain is visibly heavier than in shot 15 and the early hook frames. One part is still weak. At g1173 the coins sit as a small gold pile (about 25-30 px full size, about 12-15 px on the phone) on his chest. No hand from the crowd passes a coin up, so "coins changing hands" reads as "coachman has money" more than "people pay him". That is enough for the line "rainy days are their richest", but it is not the strongest version.
- Shot 15: the purse, the look up into the swelling rain and the grin read as "rain = money". Done.
- Shot 17: a screaming runner chasing a loaded coach through the rain reads as "everyone wants a coach, they are full". The idea of "all coaches rolling away" is carried more by the runners than by visible coach travel. It is acceptable.
- Shot 18: dry, smug Hanway with the umbrella in front of a dripping, miserable queue reads as "free vs paying" at phone size. The coachman's face changes to worried with a sweat drop at g1308-1313 while he is still in frame. At g1318+ he slides to the right edge and is cut (f_056-057).
