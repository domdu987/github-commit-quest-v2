## Reveal test: is the mechanism ONE clear picture on a phone?
No. The narration says rain makes everyone fight for coaches, coachmen get rich, and the umbrella lets people walk home free. The pieces are spread over four shots, and none of them holds the whole idea.
- Shot 15: a happy coachman with a purse. It reads as "a coachman likes money", not yet "because of rain".
- Shot 16 (the picture meant to be THE reveal): three or four people push at one coach. It reads as "some people board a coach". It does not read as "the whole street fights for coaches in the downpour". The rain does not visibly get heavier (same streak density as other takes), and no coins change hands at phone size.
- Shot 17: a quiet street and a parked coach. It reads as nothing, or even as the opposite (no customers), which steps on the ending (shot 22, the empty rank).
- Shot 18: Hanway walks past coaches. There is no paying queue or wet crowd next to him to contrast with (one small wet man), so "free vs paying" does not read, and the coachman's reaction is out of frame.

## The 3-5 most valuable changes
1. **Shot 16: make it the one mechanism picture.** Rank_rush at tile-13 density or more: 12-20 readable cards packed at two or three coach doors, overlapping, arms up, hats over heads, some climbing on the step. Pour the rain to 1.3-1.5x with a visible splash. In the same frame, the head coachman, big and upper right, holds his purse up while a hand pays a coin into it at 39.0 s (the coin clink cue), with a gold glint, coin about 15 px or more on the phone. Rain, crowd fighting and coachman cashing in all in one frame is the reveal.
2. **Shot 17: show coaches full and rolling, never an empty street.** Use the tile 15 framing with the coaches actually moving toward or across the camera, a wheel wake, faces/silhouettes crammed in the windows and on the roof, and the leftover crowd still running after them and waving coins. Coachmen b/c cheering should be visible at a readable size. If time is short, cut shot 17 to under 1 s, or merge it into shot 16 rather than ship the empty frame.
3. **Shot 18: build the contrast inside the frame.** Put a soaked queue of 5-8 cards (hunched, dripping, coins in hand) along the coach, with Hanway strolling past dry in front. Keep the head coachman in frame (upper right) through g1307 so the grin drop is seen, or add a 0.6 s insert of his face falling. Hold Hanway's turn of the head toward the queue with a small smile.
4. **Shot 15: tie the money to the rain.** On the look up at the sky, have the rain visibly swell (the plan says 0.7 to 1.0, which does not read), and let the coachman rub his hands or shake the purse (coins pop). Replace or light the two flat black lantern blocks (f_008-014) that eat about 15 % of the frame. Give them warm emissive glass like tile 09.
5. **Thunder flash at 37.2 s** (the cue is already in the plan): a one-frame cool lift on the cut into shot 16, to mark "now it pours, now the money comes".

## Verdict: FIX-FIRST
Light, sharpness and set quality are on the brand. The key moment fails, though: on a phone the mechanism (rain, then a coach fight, then rich coachmen, while the umbrella walks home free) is not one clear picture. Shot 16 is too thin, and shot 17 is an empty street, which breaks the emptiness rule. Changes 1 and 2 are the blockers, and 3 is strongly recommended.
