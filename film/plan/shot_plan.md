# Final shot plan: the Storm Expressway (65.4 s, 720x1280, 30 fps, Thai)

Director's merge of plans mirror-A, mirror-B and lean, checked and repaired by the last check (see Notes, LAST CHECK). Picture is cut to `audio/th/full_v2/narration_timing.json`; narration is laid at +0.100 s, 1965 frames. Clock: narration clock = position in narration.wav, for every time in this plan (t0, t1, snaps, acting beats, sound cues). The wav is laid at +0.100 s, so film time = clock + 0.100 and an in-shot event at clock x falls on film frame round((x + 0.1) * 30). A shot is cut 0.1 s (3 frames) ahead of its first word: its first film frame is round(t0 * 30). Every shot is true 3D (office exists; the beach, already under construction, and a small kitchen are the two new sets); the flat compositor only supplies textures and overlays.

**Counts:** 26 shots (25 takes), mean 2.52 s; 17 dissolves of 9-17 frames, 2 hard cuts, 1 whip, 1 hidden cut, 1 punch-in, 1 wipe, 1 flash; 26 snaps; a framing change every 1.26 s on average (1.32 s mean and 1.21 s median after the fixed hook).
