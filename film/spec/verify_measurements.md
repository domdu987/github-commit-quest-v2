# Independent re-measurement of LOOK_SPEC.md (v2)

Auditor pass, default distrust. 15 claims re-measured from the reference stills (`PROJ/ref/a`, `PROJ/ref/b`) and from frames I extracted myself from the two source videos into `PROJ/ref/extra/verify/`. `LOOK_SPEC.md` was not edited. No Blender render was started.

Result: 11 confirmed, 3 confirmed with a correction to part of the claim, 1 refuted. The refuted one is the rule that the thick side of the head outline and the lavender crescent always sit on the lower right.

## How I measured

- `D` (outer head diameter, ink to ink) is the unit for every character number, so I measured it first and separately for each head. Inner ink edge: flood fill of the head interior (fill plus hair, bounded by ink and hat), minimum enclosing circle, then a least-squares circle on the boundary points within 3.5 percent of it. Outer ink edge: luma half-maximum along rays every 1.5 deg from that centre. `D` = inner diameter plus the two opposite ink widths (or twice the outer circle radius when rays cover at least 45 percent of the circumference). Every fit was checked by eye on an overlay; fits that leaked were discarded.
- 30 clip A heads from 26 stills passed (a/t00.0, t03.0, t12.0, t12.5, t13.0, t13.5, t14.0, t14.5, t15.0, t15.5, t16.0, t16.5, t19.5, t20.0, t20.5, t21.0, t23.5, t27.5, t28.0, t35.0, t44.5, t45.0, t46.0 x2, t46.5, t47.0 x2, t47.5, t53.5, t54.0), plus a/t02.0 by an arc fit, plus 11 clip B heads.
- Face features: dark connected components inside the fill on a 4x upsample, threshold at luma 140, sizes are bounding boxes.
- Body: luma run lengths along rows and columns, RGB dumps across contours, and a distance-transform ridge for stroke widths.
- Colour: my own PIL + numpy implementation of the statistics (not `measure_color.py`), run on the 260 stills and again on 259 frames I pulled from the videos halfway between the stills (frame index mod 15 = 13).
- Scripts: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-github-commit-quest-v2/e8b79665-ba86-5bba-9861-f4e29c068ae7/scratchpad/verify_work/` (`batch.py`, `probe3.py`, `agg.py`, `crescent.py`, `face.py`, `ellfit.py`, `runs.py`, `stroke.py`, `colstats.py`, `news.py`, `orbit.py`).

The amount is as the spec says (thin 0.02, thick 0.07, ring centre offset 0.027 D). Only the direction is wrong: it is a property of the shot, not of the sprite. In the three frames where the sun glare is in the picture the crescent points away from it to within 21 deg. So clip A heads do carry one light-dependent term, and the spec's sentence that the characters are "unlit" and that 3D shading "fights" the look is too strong; this is weak support for the forensics lens's view that clip A characters are shaded 3D objects.

What to do in the build: make the crescent and ink direction one parameter of the puppet, set per shot from the screen-space direction away from the key light. With the spec's light kit (key from camera-left, above) the value is the spec's lower right, so nothing changes for most shots; a shot lit from the right needs the mirrored disc that section 2.7 forbids.
