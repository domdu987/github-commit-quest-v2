# LIGHT SPEC: light, shadow and depth like R9/R10 (client 2026-11-27)
The client's words: "ของเขามันดูมีการเล่นแสงเล่นเงา ดูมีมิติ" (theirs plays with light and shadow and has depth).
References: film/ref_shorts/R9_* and R10_*. Comparison image: film/ref_shorts/compare_R9_R10_vs_turtle.jpg.
Apply to every shot:
1. **Low sun.** Sunrise, sunset or night key, 5-15° above the horizon, so long shadows rake across the ground. No high midday sun.
2. **Backlight and rim.** Place the key behind the subject or 3/4 behind it, so characters and props carry bright rims against a darker background.
3. **Practical light pools.** Lanterns, braziers, fires, windows and cannon flashes throw warm pools of light, with darker falloff around them.
4. **Shadows from off-screen objects.** Off-screen trees, masts, rigging and buildings cast patterned shadows into frame.
5. **Light and dark zones.** About 30-40 % of the frame is in clear shadow, and the bright areas are where the story is. Never light the frame evenly.
6. **Haze and god rays.** A thin volume fades distant layers and shows sun shafts through rigging, smoke and trees.
Colour (measured on R9/R10):
- Saturation 0.28-0.35.
- Two-tone palette: warm orange/amber 35-45 % against cool blue/lavender 30-45 %, with all other hues together 20 % or less.
- Pastel gradient skies. No flat cyan.
Set dressing (R10): lived-in ground (individual stones or planks, mud, grass in cracks, wear) and props everywhere, all grounded and casting shadows.
Gate: send a lighting and look board to the producer before any full render.
