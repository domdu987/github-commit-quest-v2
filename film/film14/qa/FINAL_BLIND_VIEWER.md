# Final blind viewer answers

1. Hook: The whole village laughs at one farmer who has hung beehives around his maize field and claims bees can drive off a whole herd of elephants (0-6 s). It is odd that bees would be sent against elephants, and the opening frames show villagers cracking up at a man with hive boxes.

2. The loud neighbour (uncle) mocks the farmer, and the villagers follow, calling him "crazy bee guy" (13-25 s). The mocker promises that if bees can chase off elephants, he will let them sting his nose (the farmer's idea is dismissed as a bug the size of a bean scaring a 5-ton elephant).

3. The farmer strings ONE wire through every hive so they form a connected ring around the field. When an elephant pushes the wire, the whole row of hives swings and bangs together, waking thousands of bees at once (43-53 s). The bees then go for the elephant's thin skin around the eyes and inside the trunk.

4. The elephants flee because the stings hit the soft spots (eyes, trunk), since the skin there is not thick. The whole herd turns and runs, and remembers the buzzing sound. The bees swarm over the lead elephant and attack it, and stay with their hives at the farmer's field (the farmer loses no maize). They are not shown going anywhere else.

The first hive he hangs stings his nose, just as he promised at 13-22 s, and it swells up like an elephant trunk.
