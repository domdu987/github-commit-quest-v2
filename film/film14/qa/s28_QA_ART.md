## Does the payoff land on a phone?
- **Sting (shot 29): yes.** The bee card is big and gold, it is on the nose at g1990 (caption "ผึ้งก็ต่อย" 66.23 s), the nose turns red, and the uncle reacts with wide eyes, a sweat drop and both hands up. All of it reads at 180 px wide.
- **Trunk (shot 30): yes, and well timed.** The swell runs g2045 -> g2088, starts on "จนจมูกบวม" and reaches full on "งวงช้าง" (69.48 s = g2087). It ends as a long ridged trunk with two nostrils, the eyes watering, and it is the clearest silhouette in the take. The hard cut at g2104 lands on the end of the word.
- **Sheepish ask (shot 28): weakest beat.** The cues are there: sweat drop, a hand rubbing the back of his neck, the farmer smug with his hands on his hips. But the hive box is tucked under the uncle's arm and half hidden, both characters face the camera instead of each other, and at phone size his half-lidded smile looks almost as smug as the farmer's. On a phone this reads as "two men standing", not "the mocker comes to beg".

## Verdict: FIX-FIRST (small fixes; the take does not need a full re-render)
Shots 29-30 nearly SHIP as they are. Shot 28 and the continuity break hold the take back.

## Changes (at most 4)
1. **Shot 28, make the ask readable.** Turn the uncle 3/4 toward the farmer. Have him hold the empty hive box out in both hands toward the farmer, chest-high and in front of his body, not under his arm. Give him a downcast, apologetic face: brows up and together, eyes down, a small wobbly mouth, not the half-lid smile. The farmer keeps the smug grin and turns his eyes to the box.
2. **Shot 28, reframe.** Lower the camera and tilt it up (or dolly in about 25 %) so the two men's knees sit near y 700. This keeps the caption band y 880+ clear of heads but loses most of the empty soil. Then the hive fence and maize fill the frame behind them, and his trampled field is ideally visible over his shoulder. That one detail tells the viewer why he has come.
3. **Shot 30, continuity and size.** Either keep him on the stool (stool top visible at the bottom of the frame), or frame waist-up so the stool is out of shot. Waist-up is preferred: the face and trunk become about 1.5x bigger on the phone, and the trunk can hang out of frame at the bottom. Keep his heads and nose above the caption band at the start: the nose tip may cross y 880 only once it is clearly a trunk, which the caption will partly cover. Check that with QA-tech.
4. **Shot 30, finish on a gesture, not a still.** From g2088 to g2104 the trunk is static. On "งวงช้าง" give it a small elephant-like curl-up or wiggle at the tip (a 6-10 frame ease), plus a blink so the uncle is alive in the last frame. Optional extra in shot 29: in g1962-1985, have his hands lift the hive onto the bar ("พอแขวนรังแรก") before the bee comes out, so "hanging his first hive" is seen and not just implied.
