# QA_TECH s11 (shots 11-14, g 759-1000, handles 753-758 / 1001-1006) - FAIL
Reviewed at 6 fps plus every cut +-3, full-res spot frames (g 793, 838, 903, 968), 360x640 montages. qa_auto PASS is not sufficient.

1. g 821-1000 (shots 12 and 14): the bottom ~45 % (y > ~700) is a blurred, blotchy soft ground (road + grass patch with a hard blurry-edge seam, in shot 14 a straight horizontal band at y~590-640 of the sheet). It is a low-resolution ground texture/mip blur, not DOF, but it reads as out-of-focus and breaks the SHARP rule; the road, tyre track and dark "blob" shadows at the left are mush. Fix: crop/frame so the field+hives fill the frame (tilt/push in so ground below the camp < 20 %), and/or raise ground texture resolution / anisotropic filtering / disable mip bias on the road+grass-patch material; replace the blob decals with real shadows.
2. g 821-863, 930-1000: hives are tiny roofs (~50-80 px wide on 720) and the wire is a 2-3 px line, which does not read at 360x640. The 1 -> 10 -> all count is not legible; shot 12 and shot 14 look nearly the same framing and differ only by light. Fix: lower and push the ring camera so the hive row fills the width at about 2x the size (roof ~140 px), fly along the row; put a visible cue per hive (warm lamp or bee glow); in 14 make the wire thicker/glowing and run the camera over the whole loop so the wire reads as one closed line.
3. g 821-863 / 930-1000 (count): shot 12 shows about 10 hives (left side not visible, 2 back, 3 right), shot 14 shows about 16 (5 front, 5 back, 4 left, 3 right). The count change 10 -> more is ambiguous and the ring is not the same ring. Fix: use the same ring in both shots, with exactly 10 hives in 12 (all visible in frame) and the same hives, now wired, in 14.
4. g 759-820 (shot 11): camera is near-static (about 8-10 % push over 62 frames; background identical). The hive is a flat brown plank that reads as a sign, not a hive (no roof, entrance or bees visible; the entrance only shows in later shots), and the farmer never ties anything: arm held up on the beam, pose frozen for 62 frames (only the head/arm are static; no knot or rope action). "Farmer ties it on" beat is not visible. Fix: give the camera a real move (orbit 15-20 degrees or lateral dolly plus push), make the hive read (roof, entrance with a few bees, hanging ropes), animate the hang/tie (rope in hand, arm moving to the hanger), and show the hive clearly as the single subject.
5. g 864-929 (shot 13): the wire does NOT visibly thread through the hive bar. The wire is a thick pale tube running along the beam at the post tops; the hive hangs from it on two rope lines with no pass-through, and the farmer's hand rests on the post/tube without pulling. The coil in his hand stays unchanged. Fix: show the hive's hanging bar with a hole/eyelet and animate the wire tip going through it (hand moves, wire slack to taut, glint), then the wire running off to the next hive; frame the bar in the middle third at larger size.
6. g 864-929 (shot 13): farmer mouth switches between a flat line and a small open oval (g 885-927 sequence) while no quoted line is spoken; only the uncle may have a talk mouth. Fix: hold one mouth shape for the whole shot.
7. g 759-929 (stool contact): farmer is on the stool, shoes touch the top, scale ok, and he does not slide (stool and he move together, consistent). But the stool has almost no contact shadow (dark ground) and the stool top sits slightly in front of the post, with the thin plant stem passing through the shin line at g 880-929. Fix: add a soft contact shadow under the stool legs, move the stool 0.1 m back from the lens, move the sapling stem out of the leg area. (Parenting itself: OK; no float seen.)
8. g 759 / 821 / 864 / 930: cuts land on the plan; no pops, black or magenta. The day -> dusk jump at 864 is as planned. The cut 821 (close street-level to high ring view) is a large scale jump with no motivation; keep, but resolve with the move in item 4.
9. Camera move summary: shot 11 weak (see 4), shot 12 ~10 % crane (weak), shot 13 slow track + zoom (adequate, moving every frame), shot 14 ~8 % rise (weak). Fix: make shots 12 and 14 true cranes/orbits (at least 25 % change in framing).
