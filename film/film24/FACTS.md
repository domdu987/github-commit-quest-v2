# Film 24 "ไอ้เรือเต่า": facts and inventions

Series: one amazing war weapon per country, film 1 (Korea).

## Facts (checked 2026-11-26)
- 1592: Japan, under Toyotomi Hideyoshi, invaded Joseon Korea (the Imjin War), landing at Busan in April with a fleet of several hundred transports.
- Admiral Yi Sun-sin (이순신), commander of the Left Jeolla Navy, had a turtle ship (geobukseon, 거북선) built and armed just before the invasion.
- The roof was closed over the deck. Yi's own war report of 1592 mentions iron spikes on the roof, put there so enemies could not board. Whether the roof was also iron-plated is disputed; the film says only "spikes".
- A dragon head at the bow. Later accounts say it could release sulfur smoke as a screen; some versions could fire a cannon through it. The smoke detail is not from a battle account (the film shows it as a design feature only).
- Gun ports on all sides (cannons on both sides, bow and stern).
- Japanese naval tactics relied on grappling and boarding, then fighting hand to hand with swords. Korean ships fought with cannon at range.
- First combat use: Battle of Sacheon, 1592.
- Battle of Hansan Island, 1592 (usually dated 14 August): a few Korean ships pretended to flee from the narrow Gyeonnaeryang strait. The Japanese commander Wakisaka Yasuharu followed with his whole fleet (about 73 ships) into open water. Yi then turned his fleet into the "crane wing" (U-shaped, 학익진) formation, surrounded them and fired from all sides. Sources disagree on numbers; at least 47 ships were destroyed and 12 captured, i.e. more than half. Wakisaka escaped.
- Japanese survivors were stranded on Hansan Island for days (Korean accounts mention living on seaweed). Wakisaka himself escaped by boat.

## Dramatised / invented (must be marked in the caption)
- The mocking Japanese commander and both his quotes ("เรือมีกระดองเหรอ ... ข้าจะคลานกลับบ้านเอง", "เห็นมั้ย เต่ากลัวแล้ว") are invented. He is a composite cartoon, not Wakisaka; he did not see the ship being built.
- The nickname "ไอ้เรือเต่า" said by Japanese soldiers is invented (the Japanese reportedly called them "mekura-bune", blind ships).
- The ending (the commander clinging to a plank and crawling ashore) is a comic invention.
- The step-by-step order of adding spikes, dragon head and gun ports is a simplification.
