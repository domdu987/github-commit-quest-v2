## Polish notes (optional, in priority order)
1. **Reveal C (43.2-47.6 s), the miss is only just shown.**
   - The cut comes as the torpedo reaches the stern track (checked at 47.45 s), so the viewer does not see it clearly run on past the stern.
   - Let the torpedo cross the track and continue 1-2 ship-lengths before the cut. Either push the torpedo timing about 0.4 s earlier, or slowly push the camera in by about 15 % so the ship, ghost and wake fill more of the frame.
2. **The ghost's "opposite heading" is not readable from above or in the periscope.** The ghost overlaps the real ship, so it reads as "a ship further back" rather than "facing the other way". The current reading still makes sense, but a pale bow wave on the ghost's far end, or rotating the ghost 180 degrees, would make the mechanism explicit.
3. **From top-down, the dazzle ship is hard to tell from the grey ones.** The deck is brown like the others and only the striped funnels set it apart. A faint white rim highlight, or a slightly lower oblique angle so the striped hull side shows, would help.
4. **Top-down sea framing (36.4-39.8 s and 43.2-47.6 s):** there is a hazy grey band at the top of the frame and a cut-off ship sliver at the left or right edge. Trim with a slight reframe.
