## Numbers checked but NOT spoken (sources disagree)
- **Print run:** 700,000 (WP), 750,000 (LTM and Londonist snippets), "some 850,000 in the first two months of 1933" (EH). The script says "หลายแสนแผ่น" (several hundred thousand), which every source supports.
- **Trial print:** 500 copies in 1932 (WP only; LM confirms the 1932 trial without a number). The script says "พิมพ์ทดลองนิดเดียว แจกไม่กี่สถานี".
- **Beck's pay:** "thought" to be five guineas (EH); five or ten guineas, or never paid (WP); 10 guineas for the card and 5 for the poster (WP Tube map); 15 guineas (Londontopia). Not used in the narration; the caption says only "ค่าจ้างไม่กี่กินี".

## Dramatised (marked in the release caption)
- The publicity official who laughs, his two lines, and his promise to eat the map. He is invented; the real rejection came from the Publicity department, not a named person.
- The nickname "ไอ้ช่างวงจร".
- The ending (the official now has hundreds of thousands of maps to eat) is a joke.
- The pictured office, passengers and gestures are staged.
