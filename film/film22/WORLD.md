# Film 22 world: "ไอ้ช่างวงจร" (Harry Beck's Underground diagram, London 1931-33)
One NEW connected world in `film/v3/set3d22/` (never reuse an older set; helpers may be imported like set3d10 does).
Signature colours: the bold Tube-line colours **red, blue, green, yellow** (plus a little brown/black line) against a **deep dark-blue tunnel/night** and the **warm amber 1930s office**. Film saturation ≥ 0.50, vivid ≥ 45 %, mean luma 85-110 (QA §G, §I).
Light is always motivated: desk lamps and green-shaded bankers' lamps, gas/electric street lamps, the lit station entrance, platform globe lamps, train headlights and lit carriage windows, a warm sunset band low in a deep-blue dusk sky. Rim light on every character (cool blue rim indoors from the window, warm rim outdoors from lamps). Text signs ARE allowed: short English signs (UNDERGROUND roundel, station names, DRAWING OFFICE, PUBLICITY DEPT, WAY OUT, TO TRAINS).

## Locations (connected so the camera travels instead of cutting)
1. **london** — exterior, dusk turning to night (deep blue sky, warm orange horizon band, first stars).
   - A 4-5 storey brick-and-stone Underground office building (the HQ) with rows of lit windows; one named lit window: the PUBLICITY DEPT (3rd floor, big window, posters visible inside). ACROSS the street: a narrow brick lodging house with a lit attic/2nd-floor window = **Beck's lodgings** (he drew the map while laid off, at home), with a view over the street to the HQ and the station entrance.
   - The street below: an Underground station entrance with a big red-blue **UNDERGROUND roundel** sign and a lit canopy, steps going down, a pavement **ventilation grate** (iron grille) with warm light coming up, a red double-decker bus, black cabs, lamp posts, a newspaper kiosk, a flower stall, crowds in hats and coats (bowler, cloche) walking with umbrellas/briefcases.
   - Beyond: rows of roofs and chimney pots with smoke, the Thames with a bridge and reflections, a big dome on the skyline, a clock tower; city lights twinkle.
   - Needed views: **extreme wide from high** (whole city, river, the HQ findable), street level, the grate from above (camera dives through it), the windows from outside (camera pushes through).
2. **under** — the underground, connected below the grate:
   - A shaft from the grate down into a **cutaway of tangled tunnels** that coil "like noodles" (several tube tunnels in dark blue earth, crossing at odd angles, small lit trains inside) — the pivot image.
   - A curved-ceiling **platform** (cream and maroon/green tiled walls, a long colour band, globe lamps, benches, posters, a big station-name roundel e.g. PICCADILLY CIRCUS / BANK, a pinned OLD geographic map that is cramped and tangled, a WAY OUT sign, a clock). The tunnel mouths are deep dark blue with a warm headlight coming. A **red train** arrives/departs with lit windows.
   - The **ticket hall** above the platforms: ticket windows, escalator heads, a leaflet rack/stand of folded pocket maps, a kiosk, crowds.
   - A **carriage interior**: seats, straps, lit lamps, passengers, a line diagram above the windows, dark tunnel rushing past the windows.
3. **hq** — Beck's lodgings room plus the HQ interior (one connected HQ floor so the camera can follow Beck walking):
   - (NOT in the HQ) **Beck's lodgings room** at night, across the street, connected to the london exterior through its window: a small homely room (patterned wallpaper, a bed corner, a kettle, a coat on a hook, a letter marked NOTICE on the table = laid off), a tilted drafting board with the big colourful diagram in progress (straight red/blue/green/yellow lines at 0°/45°/90°, station ticks and interchange circles), T-square, set squares, ruler, ink bottles in the line colours, a green-shaded lamp, circuit diagrams pinned on a cork wall, the OLD tangled map pinned beside the board, rolled drawings, tea cup, a wastepaper basket of crumpled tries, a window to the dusk city with the HQ and the station roundel visible across the street.
   - An HQ **corridor** with doors (SIGNALS, PUBLICITY DEPT), a notice board, ceiling lamps, a stair.
   - The **publicity department**: a warm wood-panelled open office with clerks' desks, typewriters, framed Underground posters, a coat stand; the official's big desk with a **drawer**, a desk lamp, in-tray, a rubber stamp; window light shafts from the street lamps; later piles and towers of printed pocket maps.
4. **mapworld** — the diagram as a 3D world (reveal/aha): cream paper ground the size of a town, raised glossy coloured lines (red, blue, green, yellow, brown, black) running only horizontally, vertically and at 45°, round interchange rings like little plazas, station tick marks with name tags, tiny trains riding along the lines, tiny passengers walking to the rings; the camera can ride a train along a line and rise to see the whole diagram.
