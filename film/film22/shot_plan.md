## Camera grammar (client rules: QA_TEAM §G, §H, §I; production-lessons §10)
- **The camera travels instead of cutting.** 15 takes joined by TRAVEL joins (an invisible join: the next take starts on the exact last framing of the previous take, the move continues with the same speed), push-through joins (through glass, a grate, a paper), match dissolves, and only **3 hard cuts** in the film (limit 12).
- Every take moves all the time, eased: push/pull 6-10 % per second plus slide about 40 px/s; never parked > 1 s. In-shot frame-diff median ≥ 4, p90 ≥ 12 (film 2: 5.3 / 19.1).
- **BIGZOOM 1** (s02+s03, 3.55-9.30 s) and **BIGZOOM 2** (s15, 58.40-63.00 s): extreme wide from high over London (character < 5 % frame height) to a medium shot (35-60 %), passing roofs, the grate, tunnels, windows on the way.
- Angle types used: low hero (s01, s09), bird's-eye / high (s02, s07, s14, s15), top-down (s05), through a foreground frame (s06 glass partition, s09 crowd), over-the-shoulder (s11), side tracking (s08, s10), crane up/down (s02, s07, s09, s13), ride-along (s12). Never more than 2 shots in a row at the same height/angle.
- At least half the screen time wide or medium-wide showing the place. Close-ups only as punctuation (≤ 3, about 1 s each): s06 on "ทั้งแผ่นเลย!", s10 the official's jaw drop. No face taller than 35 % of the frame otherwise. Nobody cut in half by a frame edge.
- Foreground prop 1-2 D from the lens in most shots (ink bottles, lamp, typewriter, umbrella, bench, ticket barrier, strap handle).
- Motivated light and rim light on every character. Signature colours: Tube-line red/blue/green/yellow against deep blue and warm amber. Film saturation ≥ 0.50, vivid ≥ 45 %, luma 85-110. Heads above y 880; no picture text in the caption band.
- Characters always act; nobody still > 1.5 s; laughing characters bounce 0.06 D at about 3 Hz. Nothing floats: contact shadows on every foot/body.
