# Second review s01: AGREE-OVERRIDE
I extracted all 36 frames from g60 to g95 and looked at them three ways: a 180x320 phone sheet, full-size crops of the left edge (g64/72/76/80/84/88/92) and half-size full frames (g68/74/80/86/92). I also checked g40-56 and g100/130/155 against the y 880-950 band. Working files: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-github-commit-quest-v2/64d228e2-e8b3-5935-8909-70298ec15c3e/scratchpad/r2/

1. **The left-edge ox is (a), a camera-move exit.** The dragon-coated ox is a mid-ground herd extra in the back rows, about the same size as its neighbours, and not a foreground figure. The truck carries it out in one direction with no hold or pause: about 40 % of it is visible at g72, a rump at g80, a sliver at g84, and it is gone by about g87. That is about 0.5 s while the camera is moving.
2. **It does not read as a framing error at phone size.** The eye is on Tian Dan at frame centre: he is the brightest figure, has the only human face, and stands in the lantern pool. The ox reads as the herd going on past the frame, which is what a dense herd behind the hero should look like. Its head is already out of frame, so no face is ever cut.
3. **The second ox (g82-88) is minor.** Only rear legs and hooves show at x 0-30. Several of those frames are motion-soft (QA_AUTO lists g80, 82-86), so on a phone it does not read as a figure at all.
4. **§G does not fit this case.** Its "no character cut in half by an edge" targets characters framed or held across an edge. Removing every herd extra that touches an edge during a dolly through a 70-ox herd would empty the "rich set" that §H requires. The fix QA-tech asks for (cull the oxen in the left strip) is optional polish, as QA-art says.
5. **The same logic covers the right-hand herd lane at g60-68.** It passes out of the right edge in about 0.2 s, and there some ox faces are briefly cut. QA-tech did not flag this, and it reads the same way.
6. **The y 880-950 band check is not met literally, but I do not count it as a blocker.** Faces of herd oxen in the right lane do sit in and below the band from about g40 to g66 (one ox muzzle is at y ~850-935 at g60-64). No human face goes below y 880. §H only asks that the caption band be clear *at the end* of a BIGZOOM, and it is: at g100-155 only Tian Dan's and the oxen's legs cross it. The director should confirm that this is fine for herd faces mid-move.
7. **Poles:** The plaque post leaves the frame at about g70. In g60-95 no pole or post crosses a face.
8. **Ghosts:** There are no ghost duplicates and no doubled figures.
9. **The zoom reads as one continuous move.** It is a single eased crane-down and push from g0 to g108, followed by a slow push. There are no cuts, jumps or pulses in g60-95.
