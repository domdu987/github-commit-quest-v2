## Changes
1. **Flames, whole take:** Put the flame on each tail tip and have it trail backward along the motion vector, with spark particles streaming behind. No flame card may overlap an ox head. This is the beat itself.
2. **g 1520-1581 (50.56-52.59 s):** Every ox runs the same way, away from its flame. Track alongside at herd speed so a lead ox in clean profile, with fire behind it, stays at 35-50 % of frame height for the whole sentence.
3. **g 1585-1601:** Keep pace with the herd, or push in, rather than letting it recede into the distance.
4. **Composition:** Lower the camera's look-at or tilt down so the ground is at most 25 % of the frame. Lighten or break up the dark diagonal stripe shadows.
