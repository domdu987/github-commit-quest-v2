What works: the dragon silk coat pattern reads well, the scared soldier's O-mouth and sweat drop read, and the warm brazier at the right is a nice practical.

Problems (strict, centrepiece):
- It is a wide, not a close-medium. The ox takes up only about 35 % of the frame width, the sky about 30 %, and bare ground the bottom ~40 % (i0-i70). On the phone the "monster" is a small cute cow.
- The HERO OX'S TAIL IS NOT LIT. Its reed tip curls up at the top right of the ox, unlit. The only flames belong to background oxen and sit directly behind the soldier's helmet (i0, i56, i68), so the soldier reads as having his head on fire. This breaks rule 3.
- No glint on "วาววับ" (i55): the horn blades are brown brush-like tips with no metal and no highlight. The ox's face is a gentle smile, not "full flare".
- ZOOM PULSING. Three in/out pairs: i17-19 in then i23-26 out, i37-39 then i44-47, i55-57 then i59-65.
- Flat moonlit blue light. The fire casts no warm light on the ox or the soldier. The soldier faces the lens, not the ox (rule 7).

## Changes
1. **Framing, i0-i70:** make it a real close-medium. Put the camera at the ox's eye height, with the ox head, horns and front of the coat filling about 60-70 % of the width and the horizon low. Put the soldier in the left foreground in profile or 3/4, looking at the ox and frozen. Add a dark fg prop (overturned wine jar or spear rack) bottom-left. Plain ground should be 25 % or less.
2. **Flame, i0-i70:** parent a large flame card and a warm point light to the HERO tail tip, trailing backward and up behind the ox and clear of every face. Move the background oxen so no flame sits behind the soldier's helmet. Use the tail light as the key: orange rim on the coat, horns and the soldier's face, with the moon kept as a cool fill only.
3. **วาววับ, i55-i61:** make the blades steel-grey with a bright specular edge. Add a star-glint sprite on each blade tip (ramp in 2 frames, hold 2, fade 2) at i55, plus a small fire flicker on the steel. Swap the smiling head for the flaring/charging expression (glaring eyes, flared nostrils, steam).
4. **Camera:** remove all three punch-in/out pairs. Use one eased push of 9 %/s toward the ox head plus a slow slide, and at most ONE held punch-in (+15 %) at i55 that does not release before the cut.
