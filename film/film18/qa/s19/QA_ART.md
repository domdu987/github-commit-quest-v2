## Changes
1. **Flames vs heads, g 1436-1501:** There must be zero frames with a flame card or reed bundle over another ox's head or neck. Run the oxen out of the holes screen-right in near side profile (yaw within 30 degrees of profile), not 3/4-front, with at least 1.5 body lengths nose-to-tail. Each flame then streams back into clear space behind its own rump. Draw the tail stalk from the rump to the bundle so the flame is visibly attached.
2. **Purple ditch, whole take:** Give the `banks` ditch the ground dirt texture, tinted darker brown with wet-mud specks, not flat purple. Or cover it with the moat bridge planks or reed tufts in dark green-brown.
3. **Bottom of frame, whole take:** Lower the look-at about 4 degrees (or drop the camera to about 1.2 D) and put a dark fg prop low (bridge rail, reed bundle) so plain ground is 25 % or less.
