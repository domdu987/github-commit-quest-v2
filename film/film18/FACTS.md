# Film 18 — Tian Dan's fire oxen at Jimo (Qi vs Yan, 279 BC): facts and dramatisations

## Sources
1. Sima Qian, *Shiji* ch. 82, 田單列傳 — read at https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/史記/卷082 (ctext.org/shiji/tian-dan-lie-zhuan returned an anti-scraping page, so Wikisource was used for the same text).
2. Wikipedia, "Tian Dan" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tian_Dan (secondary; agrees with the Shiji on every point used).

Number of oxen: only "千餘" (over a thousand). The "exactly 1,200 oxen" seen on popular sites is NOT in the Shiji and is not used.

## Dramatised (say so in the posting caption)
- The mocking Yan general ("ขุนพลเยียน"), his laugh, both quotes and his promise are invented. He is an invented officer, not the real commander Qi Jie (騎劫), who was killed in the rout (齊人遂夷殺其將騎劫). Our ending (he is chased home by oxen) is a comic invention.
- The nickname "ไอ้เสมียนวัว" is invented.
- "สิบตัว ร้อยตัว" (the counting-up) and "from every house" dramatise 收城中得千餘牛.
- "Tomorrow we eat beef" toast is invented.
- The battle is shown comic and bloodless: oxen stampede, tents fall, soldiers scatter; the Shiji's 所觸盡死傷 (deaths and wounds) is not shown.
- Omitted for length (true): Yue Yi replaced by Qi Jie through Tian Dan's rumours; the nose-cutting and grave-digging provocations; the gold sent by Jimo's rich men.
