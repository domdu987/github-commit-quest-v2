# R11 "Why Warships Were Painted Like Zebras" vs our film 10 (zebra_v2): same topic, so this is a direct comparison
Same story formula: hook, mocker ("war or the circus?"), the hero keeps going, a test at sea, a torpedo misses, an explainer, a callback. The colour measures about the same (sat 0.40 vs 0.40). The difference is in staging:
1. **Close framing.** R11 shows characters waist-up and big in frame for most shots, with readable, expressive faces. Ours mostly uses wide shots with small figures.
2. **A new picture every line.** R11 cuts every 1-2 s, and each cut is a new composition. Ours holds the same dock wide for 00:10-00:25, and the same sea shot for 12 s (00:36-00:47).
3. **A literal visual gag per phrase.** "Circus" gets sailors as a jazz band with trumpets and a clown nose. "Catch a fly" gets a fly swatter. "Even the king" gets a king with a crown at a spyglass.
4. **Show the process.** The painter visibly paints fake waves, makes the back look like the front, then adds zigzags, one action per line.
5. **A simple explainer diagram.** A top-down chart shows the torpedo's dotted path to where the ship WILL be (ours: client rule, no text or arrow overlays, so use in-world props such as a chart table with model ships and a string line).
6. **Colour.** Neutral grey steel and white light, with strong accent colours (pink cap, navy, red). Ours was lavender sunset everywhere.
