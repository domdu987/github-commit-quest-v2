# Director's own review of all 8 reference shorts (1 frame per second, every frame viewed)
Sheets: film/analysis/sheets/<clip>_0.jpg, _1.jpg (seconds labelled).

New findings beyond the per-clip agent reports:
1. Word-literal pictures: almost every second shows the noun being spoken right now (bucket, police, frog, shampoo, clock, Inbox(0) phone,
   calendar MAY, stopwatch, certificate). The picture changes with the words, ~1 new image idea per second.
2. Props carry the plot as written signs: "P-618 BAKER-MILLER PINK 1 PINT RED + 1 GALLON WHITE EXACTLY!", "FIRST LINE IN GETS IT ALL!",
   newspaper "THE CLICKETY CLACK LINE", "CERTIFICATE OF DEATH S. HOLT", "CAREER DEVELOPMENT ROOM", "LABOR LAW" padlock, "STORM OIL" can,
   "OPENING TOMORROW", "GAMBLING: NONE". (For us: Thai text signs.)
3. Reveal changes the visual language: cutaway cross-sections (grave underground, earth layers under the city), top-down pendulum diagram,
   macro soup bowl, a car on a road as an analogy, colour-coded heat on rails (purple -> yellow). Characters often leave the frame there.
4. Faces are big: many CU/ECU where the head fills 40-70% of the width, wide lens close to the character with foreground props.
   Our films keep characters small in medium/wide shots.
5. Expressions are loud and graphic: purple rage face + steam puffs, sweat drops, X/spiral eyes, red crack lines radiating from an angry
   character, glowing eyes, smug half-lids. The hero stays deadpan/smug; the antagonist does the big faces.
6. Endings: last 1-3 s is a spectacle or an ironic image (explosion, mushroom cloud, wave on the pirates, rage face, smirking widow, door
   sign). The last word lands at the last frame; no calm hold.
7. Colour: strong complementary pairs per scene (magenta vs cyan, orange canyon vs cobalt sky, blue night vs one warm window).
   Our bakery is orange-only; it needs a cool counter-colour.
8. Light: hard sun shafts through windows, sunset backlight, glowing practicals; backgrounds SHARP (only whip-pan motion blur).
9. Captions: ONE word at a time, sentence case, white with black outline, modest size (~6% of width per letter), at ~70% height.
   Ours are much bigger (Kanit Black 88) phrases.
10. The camera literally travels: through a grave into the coffin, out of a window down the street, from a soup bowl into the ocean.
