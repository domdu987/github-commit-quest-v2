## 2. Story mechanics
- Hook (0-4.3 s): "[Taboo fact] is illegal in Japan, yet [it is everywhere]." One sentence, 14 words, fact + "yet" contradiction. Opening image shows the answer (the window) without explaining it.
- Reason withheld by drip: police do nothing (10.4), the counter refuses cash (16.4), prizes only (18.8), a stranger takes tokens outside (24). Each line adds one step toward the mechanism. The reason is explicitly postponed until 30.4.
- Who mocks: the cop (smile at 13.5, stoic looks), the clerk (permanent smile), the hero's own face (smirk at 15.5 and 23.0). No explicit insulting dialogue.
- Countable escalation: 1) win bucket, 2) counter refuses cash, 3) four prizes, 4) stranger exits, 5) hidden window. Roughly 5 steps, each 3-4 s.
- False payoff: you try to cash in, no cash; prizes instead (that is the "no" that makes the viewer feel there is a hole in the story).
- Reveal: one visual mechanism = the glowing window in a wall, shown first at 0 s, found at 27-30 s, and dissected by the high-angle shot at 36.8-40.6 (hall door left, window right, queue between). Reveal sentences are plain-language equations: "hall gives prizes" / "shop buys one".
- Ending image: cop with the "GAMBLING: NONE" tick list, all smiling; last line "on paper, nobody gambles." No question, no moral, no call to action. Loops visually to the opening alley (same lit window, same alley composition).
- Counts: 153 words; sentences about 12; average 12-13 words per sentence; shortest sentence is "You see, that window isn't part of the gambling hall." The longest is 21 words.

## 8. The 5 things that make it work
1. A one-line paradox hook with "yet" that gives an immediate unanswered question (illegal vs. everywhere).
2. A single clear visual mechanism (a lit window in a wall) that is planted in frame 1, followed through the story and shown in a geography shot, so the explanation is visible as well as spoken.
3. The viewer becomes the hero ("you win a bucket...") and does something simple so each plot step feels like a personal try that fails.
4. Constant slow camera motion plus fast cuts between very different lighting (cold neon / warm interior) so something visually new comes every 2-3 s, with a word-synced caption.
5. A deadpan, ironic closing image (cop with "GAMBLING: NONE" log, everyone smiling) that restates the rule in one line and loops to the first frame.
