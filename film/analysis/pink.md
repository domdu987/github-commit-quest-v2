## 2. Story mechanics
- Hook (0-3 s): past tense flat statement of an absurd FACT ("They painted the jail cell pink") + escalation clause ("And not just any pink") = 13 words/3.1 s. No question, no teaser, just a weird noun.
- Reason withheld: whole video. Narration never explains why pink for 52 s; the audience is told it WORKS before it is told WHY. Explanation lives in the last 13 s.
- Mockers: guards at 11-14 s ("Barbie's gonna love it") and the skinny guard; mockery is mild/comic, not a bully.
- Countable escalation: 0.25 min rule "15 minutes" -> inmate 1 at 15 (calm) -> nation-wide (scale) -> the forgotten guy at 15 / 20 / "door" (three timestamps).
- False payoff: arm wrestle at 33-36 s, "two seconds". Then "Wait, what?" is the viewer's thought.
- Reveal: one visual mechanism = a stopwatch plus a time-limit. Reveal image is the rubble cell (52 s), then the knitting inmate; the final line flips the explanation: same pink, longer exposure = opposite effect.
- Ending: stopwatch + angry face, red cracks, which echoes the 26 s big-man image (loop ending).
- "You": only "You see," 52.7. Narrator is third-person and dry.
- Words 237, sentences ~22 (avg 10.8 words), shortest line "Wait, what?" (2 words). Info density is high because lines are cut to one clause.

## 8. The 5 things that make it work (ranked)
1. A single visual device (stopwatch + 15 minutes + pink) that makes the reveal one image: time-in-colour, with explanation withheld for 52 s.
2. Fast, flat, deadpan narration with a hook that is an absurd fact in 3 s ("They painted the jail cell pink. And not just any pink.").
3. Countable escalation with three clock stamps (15 / 20 / door) plus a false payoff (arm wrestling) so viewers think they have the answer.
4. Colour-coded story: cool blue world vs saturated pink cell; menace shown with red cracks, so the plot is readable without sound.
5. Constant camera motion, ~1.9 s average framing, ECU inserts and big spatial jumps (aerial) that keep it from feeling like a static cartoon; word-by-word captions for pace.
