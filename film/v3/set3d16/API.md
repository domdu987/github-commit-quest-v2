# v3/set3d16/telegraph.py : film 16 set ("ไอ้บ้าลวด", Samuel Morse's telegraph line, 1825-1845)

Real 3D Blender set (bpy 5.x, Cycles CPU), grown from the client-approved style board (`styleboard/sb.py`, board.jpg): warm low sun + blue-violet sky ambient outdoors, no post recolour (only the board's sat 1.08 compositor grade), warm cream / gilt / crimson chamber, painterly two-tone noise materials, dense props. **No depth of field anywhere. No text anywhere** (portraits are painted, the register tape has dots and dashes only).

Files: `telegraph.py` (API, lights, presets, hooks, render), `telegraph_kit.py` (builder, materials, wet group, props), `tg_line.py`, `tg_studio.py`, `tg_chamber.py` (locations), `telegraph_tex.py` (painted textures, cache `tex_telegraph/`), `tg_hue.py`, `make_contact.py` (+ `contact.jpg`, `contact_small.jpg`, `hue_report.txt`, `cost_report.txt`, `telegraph_work/final/*.png`), `t_anim.py` (hook test, prints ALL OK, 83 checks).

## Use (one process builds one location; as film8 Take / harbour.py)
```python
import telegraph as TG
A = TG.build(None, 'line_close', light='AFTER_RAIN')   # location comes from the preset; light optional; resets the scene
TG.setup_render(scene, 'final')                         # 720x1280, 'preview' 360x640; Cycles CPU, 6-7 spp + OIDN, no diffuse bounce, 2 threads
cam = TG.make_camera(A['cam'])                          # 22 mm, sensor 36, DOF OFF, clip 0.05..2000   (TG.apply_dof is a no-op)
act = TG.placeholder_actor('morse', A['marks']['morse_ladder'], face_to=cam.location)   # roles morse mocker cornell worker messenger clerk congress; HEIGHT dict
TG.clear_keys()      # removes every key the hooks made (objects, lights, nodes, groups, world, scene); keeps drivers (none used)
TG.STATE             # live dict: STATE['sun'], ['poles'], ['train'], ['marks'], ['light'] ...
```
Hooks key when `frame=n` is given (LINEAR; visibility keys are CONSTANT); without `frame` they set the value now. **Key every hook on every rendered frame** (once a hook is keyed an un-keyed call is overridden). `TG.render(path)` -> (wall s, cpu s). `TG.head_pixels(cam_name)`, `TG.stage_placeholders(A, cam_name, cam_loc)`. CLI: `python3 telegraph.py final|stills [preset[:LIGHT]]`.

## World
1 unit = 1 D = head, adult 3.0-3.4 D (`TG.HEIGHT`). Authoring in sb metres, **D = 1.85 x metres** (`SC`). Z up, ground z = 0.
**LINE** (the whole film-16 outdoors): the road, the rail and the pole line run along **world +x** (pole 0 is a hidden pole behind the hero camera so the wire arrives from the left; the hero pole is index 1) (the sb1 camera looks along +x; camera-right = -y). Road centre y ~ -2.3 (x 0..260 D), poles 6.3 D right of... on the camera-right side (y ~ -9), railway track at y ~ +13, pole row x = 7.4 + 16.65 k (16 poles, k 0..15, 5 m = 9.25 D high, bottle neck 1.06 D). Furrow beside the road (x 3.7 .. 115 D, y ~ -6.4), spool/wagon/key crate at the furrow end (x ~ 100-121), station platform on the rail side x 63..115 (z 1.85), farm and barn far +x, trees ring, hills ring R 460-700 D. The cut-away **pit** (trench_cut) lies at (96, -22.2 .. ) in the field away from every other preset (mark `pit_ground`), ground has a real hole there.
**STUDIO**: room x -7.4..7.4, y -4.8..13 (+y into the room), height 8.9; tall window on the back wall (x -1.5), door in the right wall (x 7.4, y 5.7), outside: long road + rooftops far below, fields, hills.
**CHAMBER**: from sb2: back wall with 7 windows at y 63, tiers of desks around (0, 13), front table (0, 7.8), telegraph on it, crimson carpet, chandelier; new: speaker's panelled **door-screen** (doorway) behind the dais at y ~ 25 (Morse peeks there), the mocker's stool beside the register, hats/inkwells/quills on the desks.

## Lights (`build(..., light=)`, `TG.apply_light(name, frame=None)`, `TG.set_light_mix(a, b, k, frame=None)` keys sun, sky ramp, ambient, hills, clouds, exposure, lamps, wet)
LINE: **DAY** (sb1: warm key 24 deg, ambient #4454FF), **DUSK** (key 12 deg, orange, long shadows), **RAIN** (overcast, soft sun, grey-violet sky, wet 1, puddles on), **AFTER_RAIN** (sb3: key from the other side, wet 0.9, puddles). STUDIO: **DAY** (warm window key + blue-violet window fill). CHAMBER: **DAY** (sun shafts, chandelier + sconce candles) and **NIGHT** (only candles + desk candles, blue-violet moon key and window fill, shafts off). `set_light_mix('DAY','DUSK',k,frame)` is the shot 14 DAY->DUSK blend (all numbers interpolate). Sun, ambient and sky are the only light sources outdoors: shade is blue-violet from the ambient. **Wetness is separate and keyable**: `set_wet(k, frame)` (poles, wood, road, ground, grass darken and gloss; puddles appear above 0.35).

## Hooks (LINE)
- `set_poles(state, count=None, frame=None)`: `'none'|'bare'|'bottle'`, count 0..17 from the camera end. bottle = peg + OVERSIZE (3.3x) inverted translucent green beer bottle (sun-glint star on poles 1-3) with the wire wrapped once round the glass and draped over it; bare = wire on the wood. Birds sit on the wire.
- `set_ladder(on, pole=0|3, frame)`: ladder LEANING on the hero pole (foot ~1.2 m from the pole base; the stand mark `morse_ladder` is rung 12, z 6.4 D) / on pole 3.
- `set_trench(k, plough=None, frame)`: furrow length 0..1 (open brown furrow with spoil ridges; shader clip, smooth; **0 hides it completely**, the presets line_wide / line_rail / line_close start at 0 with no pipe). set_trench also refreshes the plough marks in STATE['marks']. The plough (beam, share, handles, whiffletree + traces) stands at the front (plough=None), a float puts it elsewhere, False hides it; hidden at k 0 and 1. `TG.plough_marks(k)` -> marks mule_a, mule_b, cornell, worker_a, worker_b, morse_trench that follow it.
- `set_pipe(k, frame)`: grey lead pipe laid in the furrow 0..1 (clip; lead = warm-biased dark grey #5e5a50 with a small self-light #665e50 x1.0, so it stays grey under the blue ambient) + pipe reel on a cart at its front while 0<k<1.
- `set_train(x, frame=None, speed=None)` (line_rail looks from the FAR side of the track: wheels, rods and the steam chain are in view; the steam is a chain of 12 soft shrinking puffs, never one sphere): x = world x of the loco's front buffer (D); `'home'` = sb1 spot; None hides. The train runs toward **-x** (toward the sb1 camera): wheels roll with the distance, connecting rods turn, 10 smoke puffs billow and trail (speed from the previous call).
- `set_rain(on, frame)`: bool or 0..1 (layers); three depth layers of real streak geometry (about 1500 streaks) spread over the whole frame, placed under the camera heading at call time (call it after make_camera, and per frame with `frame=` for a moving camera: it keys the placement and the fall). Pair with light RAIN (wet, puddles).
- `set_leak(where, intensity, frame)`: `'pipe'` (18 solid cyan drips, no glow rings, stretched 3x along the fall, from the lower lip of the torn pipe down into the pit soil; trench_cut: a slightly high three-quarter view at a cut face of damp brown strata, the round grey lead pipe across the frame torn open in the middle into a lens-shaped mouth with short curled lead lips, the copper wire inside with ragged bands of cream cloth, 9 cream rags hanging from the lower lip, 2 small cyan lights on the soil, warm spot fill; the near pit wall is hidden from the camera) | `'pole'` (12 small beads crawling down the wet wood of the hero poles). Phase follows the frame: key per frame.
- `set_pulse(s, frame)`: ONE glowing pulse: a continuous tapered emissive tube sheathing the wire (24 segments, 1 m = 1.85 D behind the head, 1.6 -> 1.05 x the wire radius; white-hot only at the head, cyan along the trail, the last 30 % fades out), a hot head with a 2.2x soft cyan glow, and a cyan glow inside the glass round the head (shader term in the bottle glass, node group `TG_PULSE`) plus a small cyan light linked to the bottle glass only (the pole below stays dark). Over a bottle the head slides off the wire INTO the glass (centre of the wire wrap). s 0..1 along the whole wire (path follows the current pole state); s < 0 hides it; keyed per frame with `frame=` (tube segments key location / quaternion / scale). `TG.pulse_s_at_pole(i)` parks it in the glass of pole i (bottle_macro uses pole 1 and lowers the glass self-glow to 0.35 so the cyan wins), `TG.pulse_s_at_y(y_m)` puts it at a position along the line (line_rail: y 23.5, ahead of the train).
- `set_buggy(x, frame)`: open 1840s buggy on the road at world x (D), wheels turn; None hides. `TG.buggy_marks(y_m)`.
- wagon with the crate of 8 empty green beer bottles (4 x 2, standing apart, low crate rim so shoulders + necks stand free, clear transmissive green glass `glass_crate` without self-glow, sun glints + rim highlights on 4 necks) + a low wire coil + key crate, ladder, key + register crate at the furrow end, coil, station platform: static props (marks below).

## Hand props (how render workers do them)
The set only provides WORLD props. Everything a character holds is drawn by cast16 in the cards: **palette and brush, the sealed letter, the empty beer bottle, the candle, the telegraph key / the money in hand, the mail-bag sash and key ring**. Place the card at the stand mark; the hand lands on a world point you pick (bottle crate neck of line_wagon, the register key on the crate, the ladder pole top, the stool candle). World props that exist on the set: crate of 8 green bottles (wagon), key + register crates (furrow end and wagon tail), wire coil, spool, pipe reel, oval portrait + letter (side table), palette on the floor (`set_palette`), money sack (`set_moneybag`), mail sacks (`set_mailsacks`), tape (`set_tape`). The NIGHT table pose has a warm point light at the mocker's hand height (stool candle) so a cast candle sits in a light pool.
