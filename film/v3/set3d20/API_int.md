# set3d20 interior: CAFE + RESTO (`int20.py`)

The interior half of the film-20 set. Built on `core20.py` (materials, `MB`, sky / sun / lamps, render setup, camera, placeholder actors, keying) and on the QA-approved board code (`styleboard/sb.py`: tower lattice, Haussmann blocks, Trocadero, Seine, bridge, bentwood chair, palm, plane trees). `set3d20.build('cafe_*' | 'resto_*', light)` calls `int20.build(preset, light)`. bpy 5.3, Cycles CPU, 8 spp + OIDN, diffuse bounce 1, **no depth of field, no text anywhere** (newspapers and the protest sheet are greeked: rows of ink dashes on paper, signatures are scribbled strokes).

```python
import sys; sys.path.insert(0, 'film/v3/set3d20'); import int20 as I, core20 as C, bpy
A = I.build('cafe_low', light='DAY', view='stub')   # 'stub' (default) | 'full' = the finished tower in the window (shots 16-17)
C.setup_render(bpy.context.scene, 'final'); cam = C.make_camera(A['cams'][A['cam_name']])
C.stage_placeholders(A, A['cam_name'])               # cast20 stand-ins at the marks (contact sheet / tests)
A['hooks']['set_window_view']('full', frame=n); A['hooks']['smoke'](0.8, frame=n); A['hooks']['add_signature'](12, frame=n)   # CAFE hooks (also module functions I.set_window_view ...)
```
`A = {'preset','light','loc','cam_name','cam','cams','marks','marks_m','staging','hooks','P','state'(= core20.STATE)}` (+ `'window_world'` for RESTO). `A['cams'][name] = {loc, look_at, lens 22[, shift]}` and `A['marks'][name] = (x, y, ground_z)` are in **world D** (1 D = head, 1 m = 1.88 D = `core20.SC`); `A['marks_m']` is the same in metres. Authoring is in metres under the scaled `core20.root()`; the lights are authored in metres (`core20.lamp` scales the power). `int20.PRESETS` and `int20.TILES` (12 tiles for `make_contact.py`) are exported. One location per process (build 25-50 s; a render is 25-50 s on the shared machine, see int_hue.txt).
Test: `python3 int20.py final|draft <preset ...>` (`MEASURE=1` adds the numbers of int_hue.txt, `CAM=x,y,z,lx,ly,lz[,shift]` overrides the camera in metres, `TAG=_x` suffixes the file), `int_work/runall.sh final` (all 8), `python3 int_work/t_hooks.py` (hook test, prints ALL OK, 12 checks), `python3 int_work/make_hue.py` (writes int_hue.txt).

## Lights
`DAY` only, for both locations (the shot plan needs only day; 1887 vs 1889 differ by the window view, not the light). CAFE DAY: warm key (sun, 33 deg elevation) coming through the window into the room, cool window fill (area light in the window plane, colour `#2a4cc8`), a soft warm room fill, gas lamps (sconces, 2 chandeliers) as real point lights + emissive globes (`i_lamp`, halo from the grade), ambient `#5a6cff`. RESTO DAY: sun (25 deg elevation) through the side windows, cool window fills `(0.14, 0.30, 0.78)` in all windows, gasoliers + table lamps, ambient `#4a64ff`. Per-preset exposure / sky strength (`PRESET_P`): the wide is exposed for the room, the bright sheet and the window views are pulled down. Paris outside the windows is darkened 0.5-0.6 (`dim_objects`) so the interior sun does not clip it. `C.apply_P` / `C.lerpP` work on `A['P']`.

## Hooks (CAFE; all key when `frame=n` is given: CONSTANT for visibility, LINEAR for numbers; key every rendered frame)
* `set_window_view(view='stub'|'full', frame=None)`: `'stub'` = the tower at stage 0/1 (the four legs and the first-platform girders, 190 m away, thick members) on the skyline; `'full'` = the finished tower (430 m away, thick 2.4, fills the window height). Shots 6-10: stub; 16-17: full. `build(..., view='full')` starts in full.
* `smoke(v, frame=None, t=None)`: forge smoke from the stub, **v 0..1**: a column of 14 dark puffs that rises like a black factory chimney (150 m at v 1, puffs grow and drift, wobble follows `t` seconds, default `frame / 30`); hidden when the view is `'full'`. Shot 8: ramp v 0 -> 1 over 17.10-18.96 s.
* `add_signature(n, frame=None)`: reveals the first n of 24 scribbled signatures (0 = blank sheet with its greeked preamble, 24 = full). Keep calling it per frame (n may be fractional-floor'd by the caller).
* RESTO has no hooks. `C.clear_keys()` clears every key.
