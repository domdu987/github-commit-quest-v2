## Verdict: FIX-FIRST
The look, set, rhythm and reaction faces are shippable. But this is the payoff of the mechanism, and the punch object (the empty bottle) cannot be seen at any size, while the "every number matches" line has no readable image. Here are three fixes on the existing stage, with no new set work.

## Fixes (in priority order)
1. **Shot 28: present the bottle (g1579-1650).**
   - Pin the card to Bertillon's hand anchor (as in 27), raised to about Dupont's head height, between Bertillon and Dupont. No forearm crossing the face of the card.
   - Scale the card about 2x, to at least 220 px wide at full res at g1590.
   - Turn it about 30-40° toward Dupont while it stays readable to camera, as the plan asks ("card turned toward dupont").
   - Make the bottle panel fill the name panel. The bottle silhouette should be at least 60 px tall at full res, dark outline on a warm glowing panel (not a cyan square), lit from g1588.
   - Slide Bertillon about 0.4 D camera-left/toward the lens so he is not cropped. Aim the push-in's first second at the card (card + Dupont's face in frame), and only then the 1600-1614 snap onto the caught face.
2. **Shot 27: make "every number matches" a row-by-row read and free Dupont's face (g1509-1578).**
   - Lower the card and move it camera-left, so the portrait panel sits beside Dupont's face at eye level with no overlap on his beret/brow.
   - Keep the five rows as separate stripes with dark gaps. Each row lights on its beat (1527/1536/1545/1554/1563) and STAYS lit, with a green tick or a bright "=" mark at the row end. No single merged band at g1563+.
   - The final state at g1569 should show five distinct lit rows + five ticks.
3. **Shot 28: seat Dupont (g1579-1700).**
   - Drop his pelvis onto the seat and bend the knees, so the shins come forward in front of the seat's front rail and the feet sit on the footrest/floor ahead of the chair.
   - If the rig cannot bend, frame the legs behind the front rail/desk foreground so no straight leg shows below the seat.

Non-blocking: the inspector's mouth could open a bit more on the shock beat (a dark oval about 25 px tall, under the moustache, at g1607 and g1656+). Keep it silent/closed outside the lip-sync windows.
