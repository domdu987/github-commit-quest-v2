## Verdict: FIX-FIRST
The look and the rhythm are shippable. Shot 20 nearly works. Shot 21 does not yet perform the line: the narrator says "the inspector shouts", and the picture shows a man laughing at the camera with his hands on his belly. The false payoff needs one clear image of a mocking shout aimed at a broken Bertillon. All three fixes are pose, rig or placement changes on the existing stage, with no new set work.

## Fixes (in priority order)
1. **Shot 21 inspector: make it a shout AT Bertillon (g1128-1195).**
   - Turn his head and torso about 25-30° toward Bertillon (camera-left), and point his pupils at Bertillon.
   - Raise his camera-left hand cupped beside his mouth. Optionally, point the other hand at Bertillon on "ไอ้ช่างวัดหู" (about g1175-1195).
   - Make the speaking mouth an open dark shape that shows clearly BELOW the moustache, at least about 40 px tall at full res. Keep the lip-sync window as is.
   - Swap the closed-eye tear face for open, wide, gleeful eyes with raised brows during the shout. Keep the laugh-tears for the gendarmes only.
2. **Shot 21 staging: give the mockery a target.**
   - Separate Bertillon from the group. Move him about 0.5 D further camera-left and toward the lens, so there is a clear gap of carpet between him and the laughers. That also ends the head overlap with gendarme_a at g1164-1184.
   - Have him flinch on the shout start (head drops about 10°, shoulders sink, at about g1130-1140), and hold the slump to the end.
   - Turn both gendarmes' heads toward Bertillon while they snicker. If the rig allows, gendarme_b points at him.
   - Start the push slightly tighter, or speed the first 0.5 s, so the inspector is at least about 2x his g1129 size by g1140.
3. **Shot 20 despair and "two months".**
   - Slump Bertillon: lower his head about 10°, round his shoulders, and make the head shake slower and wearier.
   - Add a pile of rejected cards on the desk and a few spilled cards on the floor by the stool. This makes "overflowing / no match" readable at phone size.
   - Remove the white needle ray from the desk lamp.

Non-blocking: the floating half-pulled drawers read as boxes stuck to the wall. They are acceptable, but would read better as a few drawers jammed open with cards sticking out.
