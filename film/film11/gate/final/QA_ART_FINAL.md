## Verdict: FIX (one cheap fix), otherwise ship-ready
### FIX 1 — s24 (49.50-52.00, barn_door / A), all frames
The aha beat ("หมอไม่ได้รักษาโรค เขาสอนร่างกาย...") plays over the sparsest frame in the film: Jenner is a small figure (head ~60 px
full size, ~30 px on the phone) in the middle of an empty barn floor, top ~30 % bare roof with dust motes, lower ~45 % plain straw
with one stool, the door view is a flat field of trees. It breaks the client's hard rule (dense set, lower third not empty, subject
readable at phone size) at the moment that should feel like a triumph.
How: move the camera ~2 m closer / lower the look-at so Jenner's head is ~150-180 px at y ~520-600 and he fills the door frame
against the gold light; dress the near floor with what the barn already has (pails, milk churns, hay bales, a hen or two at the
edges, Blossom's flank or head at one side); keep the slow push and the sun shafts/dust. Same take length, no other changes.

No other fixes: everything else below is a note.
