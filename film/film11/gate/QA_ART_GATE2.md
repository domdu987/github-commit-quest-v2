## Per take

### s02 — PASS (note)
Hook action reads: Jenner's lancet on the red dot on James's arm, James winces then smiles. Notes (not blocking): bare lawn + fence-shadow
spikes in the lower-left third (y 900-1280, x 0-400) — if re-rendered for any other reason, put a bucket/hens/flower pot there; the red dot
is a glossy bead (bauble) rather than a scratch, acceptable at phone size.

### s13 — FIX (small)
1. A multicoloured square card stands UPRIGHT beside James's head (full-size f50 approx x 75-190, y 500-600, between the window and his face)
   — it reads as a stray sign/panel next to the key face. Remove/hide it, or lay it flat on the bed (it looks like the quilt/pillow overlay
   card rotated 90°).
2. Window: hide or darken the green hill and orange panel visible in the lower-left panes (deep blue-violet plate over the whole window, or
   a moon), and drop the room fill ~30 % so the candle pool on the mother's face and James's pillow is the brightest warm area.
   (Cheap, same render as fix 1; if only one can be done, do fix 1.)

### s15 — PASS
Golden morning, James leaps off the bed and runs grinning at camera, mother and Jenner beaming, cat and rug in the foreground. Sore card
no longer floats. Contrast with s13 works.

### s26 — FIX
The beat must read "a LONG queue of villagers to Jenner's hut, nobody grew horns". At phone size the queue is a thin row of ~6 heads
(~20 px) behind the picket fence in the upper-middle band; the hut is cut at the left edge and the lower 55 % is dirt lane with hens,
which are the biggest, most active subjects. It reads as "chickens on a lane", not "a queue".
1. Re-stage the queue as a diagonal that recedes from near camera (front right, heads ~70-90 px full-size at the front) back to the hut door
   (left, mid-ground) — camera on the lane side of the gate or inside the garden, ~1.4 m high, looking along the line. Use 9-12 villagers
   (repeat villager cards with mirror/costume tint) so the line runs out of frame at the near end = "long".
2. Move the hens to the frame edges / lower corners (keep 1-2 as dressing), so nothing in the lower third competes with the queue; keep
   all heads clean (no horns) and Jenner + lancet visible at the hut door at the far end.

### s27 — FIX (small)
Towndoc (wig, red coat) reads sheepish (closed eyes, blush, small smile) and his hand works at the left cuff; nice. But:
1. The pockmarked moustache villager (c) stands IN FRONT of him at frame right, bigger than the towndoc and cut by the edge — he steals the
   shot. Move villager c behind the towndoc (queue order: Jenner/James at the hut, towndoc next, then c, a, e receding) so the towndoc is
   the biggest, unblocked figure.
2. Make the sleeve roll visible: on the tug (g 1740-1780) show the cuff turning back to a cream rolled cuff and bare forearm (as James's
   sleeve in s02), held to the end of the take; if possible he holds his tricorn in the other hand (hat_off is not readable now).

### s28 — FIX
The final gag — Blossom "looks to the lens as if she knows the word is hers" before the hard cut on "วัว" — does not land: she ends in
pure profile looking screen-left, head jammed against the left frame edge, half the frame empty straw.
1. Turn her TO the lens over g ~1846-1858: yaw the cow card ~30-35° around its vertical axis so the head end swings toward the camera
   (three-quarter, the eye and both ears face the viewer), and dolly/pan so her head ends near frame centre (head centre x ~330-400,
   y ~560-640), with free space in front of the nose. Hold still from g ~1860 to the cut.
2. Final expression: eye open and round on the turn, then the knowing half-lid + ear flick on "วัว" (g 1866-1871) — currently the
   half-lid is there from the start and reads sleepy. Optional: tilt the camera down/closer so the empty straw floor shrinks (hens and
   pails in the lower band, cow head bigger).
