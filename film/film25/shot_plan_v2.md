# Film 25 "ไอ้ถังน้ำ": shot plan v2 (R11 lessons; replaces v1). 69.75 s, 30 fps, N = 2093, g = round(t*30), g1 exclusive
## Rules
- **Cuts and framing**
  - HARD CUTS ONLY, with a NEW composition every 0.5-2 s. Never reuse a setup back to back.
  - CLOSE framing (waist-up, faces big and readable) for every dialogue and reaction beat. WIDE only where marked W: the establishing shots and the big hero moments.
- **Gags and nouns:** one literal VISUAL GAG per phrase where marked GAG. Every spoken noun is on screen.
- **Process:** the build-up shows a worker's action that lands on its spoken word.
- **Explainers:** use in-world props only: a chart table with a model tank and a model trench, or the engineer's chalk board with a chalk drawing.
- **No text anywhere.** No overlays except the narration captions.
- **Character colour:** strong accent colours on the characters (cast25 accents v2).
- **Mouths:** the general only, inside his quotes (18.39-25.35 and 44.37-45.60 s).
- **Close-up safety:** heads above y 880. In close shots the waist-up framing crops the legs at the frame bottom, below the caption band. No face fills the frame for more than about 1 s; a waist-up shot is OK.

Framing key: W = wide, M = medium (knees-up), C = close (waist-up), I = insert (an object close-up).
