## Polish notes (optional, not blocking)
1. 47.1-51.8 s, aha shot: it is lit as bright midday with a blue sky, between two battle-dusk sequences, so the cut back to dusk at
   51.8 is a small time-of-day jump. If time allows, grade it to the planned dawn (warmer and lower sun, a little smoke). Optionally,
   add a slow dissolve to a mossy "today" version of the same wall on "ทุกวันนี้" (~50 s).
2. 38.4-47.1 s, reveal macro: it is a 3D lit scene, but the flat red brick faces and the straight yellow X-lattice of the web lean a
   little toward a diagram. More brick texture and bevel variation, and a less geometric, more fibrous web, would keep it more clearly
   "inside the world".
3. 54.0-55.0 s, "ร้าวทั้งแถว": shown on the wall-top shot of the laughing general, but no cracks are visible at his feet. A crack line
   running along the parapet, or a little shake, would put the beat in that shot too. It is already partly shown at 52-54 s.
4. 29.6-32 s, rain: the "still soft" rice mortar is hinted only by the white trough. A wet sheen or a sagging joint on Lin's section
   would make the false payoff more explicit.
