1. Hook (0-5s): The narration says one man made a six-storey brick hotel rise out of the ground while guests inside kept sipping tea. The picture shows a big brick hotel with a tiny man in a bowler hat at street level, then a bowler-hatted man and a woman in a window, then two full teacups. The strange part is the claim itself, a whole building lifted with people inside who notice nothing. The picture sets up the hotel and the tea but does not show anything rising in these 5 s, so the strangeness comes from the words, not the image.

2. The rich man in the top hat and green checked coat (shown laughing in his penthouse at about 20-24s) mocks the bowler-hatted man who proposes lifting the hotel. He says: "If it moves even one finger, I will pay the room bill for the whole hotel." The crowd then nicknames the bowler-hatted man "Jack" (ไอ้แม่แรง, "Screw-Jack guy").

3. Mechanism (30-56s): He brings 5,000 screw jacks under the building and hires 500 workers at 10 jacks each. On a whistle all 500 turn their jacks together by half a turn. The building rises a tiny bit each time, evenly at every corner, so no wall cracks and the tea does not ripple. The idea is that many tiny synchronized steps lift the whole building, where no giant machine could. It is shown with rows of jacks under the floor, workers cranking, and the tea staying still.

4. Ending (56-66s): On day 5 the rich man steps out the door. The building is almost 2 m up, but the street in front has not been filled in, so there is no floor under his feet. He falls into the mud and has to pay the hotel bill, as he said himself. It is funny because he mocked the plan and was proven wrong, then the hole he fell into is the same mud problem as the start. His own bet traps him, and the pictures show him dazed in the mud pit while the jack man stands over him holding a jug.
