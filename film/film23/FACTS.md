# Film 23 "ไอ้กองทัพลูกโป่ง" (the Ghost Army): fact sheet
Web-checked 2026-11-20. Only sourced numbers go in the script. Invented (dramatised) parts are listed at the end and marked in the caption.

| # | Fact used in the script | Number used | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The 23rd Headquarters Special Troops ("Ghost Army") was a US Army unit of about 1,100 men, many of them artists, designers and sound men. Its job was to impersonate other, larger US units. | ราว 1,100 คน | ghostarmy.org/about/23rd-headquarters-special-troops ; history.com/articles/ghost-army-world-war-ii ; nga.gov/stories/articles/ghost-armys-artists-war |
| 2 | Activated at Camp Forrest, Tennessee, on 20 January 1944. Worked in France after D-Day (June 1944). | 1944 (caption only) | ghostarmy.org/about/history |
| 3 | Tools: inflatable rubber tanks, trucks and artillery; recorded sound effects played from speakers on trucks; fake radio traffic; fake unit insignia and markings. | – | nationalww2museum.org/war/articles/ghost-army-canvas-and-camouflage ; history.com |
| 4 | Sound could carry up to about 15 miles (about 24 km). | "ไกลถึงยี่สิบกว่ากิโล" | 99percentinvisible.org/episode/show-of-force ; history.com ("could be heard 15 miles away in some instances") |
| 5 | An inflatable M4 Sherman decoy weighed about 93 lb (about 42 kg). | "สี่สิบกว่ากิโล" | slate.com (Beyer & Sayles, The Ghost Army of WWII) ; militarymachine.com |
| 6 | Anecdote: two Frenchmen on bicycles saw four GIs pick up a "40-ton Sherman tank"; the GIs told them Americans are very strong. Veterans' recollection (one veteran says two men). We use the common version: four men, "40-ton". | 4 คน, 40 ตัน | smithsonianmag.com/history/when-an-army-of-artists-fooled-hitler-71563360 ; wwiimemorialfriends.org ; euronews.com (2019 veteran interview) |
| 7 | Operation VIERSEN, 18-24 March 1945: the unit's largest deception, to hide the time, place and strength of the US Ninth Army's Rhine crossing. It simulated the 30th and 79th Infantry Divisions (two divisions). | มีนาคม 1945, สองกองพล | ghostarmy.org/thearchive/official-history-of-the-23rd-operation-viersen ; nationalww2museum.org |
| 8 | About 600 inflatable dummies were used in Viersen. | หกร้อย | nationalww2museum.org ("the 23rd and its 600 inflatable dummies") |
| 9 | The deception site was about 10 miles south of the real crossing. | "ทางใต้ของจุดข้ามจริง" (no number spoken) | history.com ; warhistoryonline.com |

Not used (could not source firmly or contested): lives-saved figures (15,000-30,000), number of men in Viersen "30,000/40,000", real Sherman weight (sources say 30 or 40 t; we only quote the anecdote's "40 ตัน" as what the cyclists saw).

## Dramatised (invented) parts, marked in the caption
- The mocking officer ("ผู้พันหน่วยข้างๆ") and his quote and promise are invented.
- The ending (the officer blowing up balloon number 601) is invented and comic.
- The cyclists' exact words and the soldier's dry smile are dramatised from the veterans' anecdote.
No combat deaths are shown or mentioned. Bloodless and comic.
