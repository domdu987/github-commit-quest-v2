## Whole-film checks

**Continuity**
- Dick's kit A (maroon and gold vest, white shorts) is the same through 0-31 s.
- His kit B (white vest with red and blue stripes, navy shorts) is the same through 35-58 s.
- The coach wears his maroon windbreaker and cap in 1963, a straw hat and flower shirt in the 1968 stands (costume B; the plan said a pennant hat, which is fine), and the windbreaker and cap again at the ending.
- The bar is red and white in both eras.
- Time of day follows the plan: GOLDEN, DAY at 30-31 s, EVE, TENSE, EVE, DAY at 60.5-64.5 s, then GOLDEN.
- Minor: the 1963 standards are grey metal, while board sb1 shows wood. This is consistent within the film, so it is not an error.
- Minor: in shot 31 (58.2-60 s) the coach stands at ground level beside the podium instead of in the stands, and his jaw-drop is small.

**Heads below y 880**
- Only shot 28, about 52.6-52.9 s. The rival's oversized head drops to about y 905 during the dissolve. This is the known pending fix.
- No other head crosses the line. I checked 12, 26, 33, 59.5 and 63.5 s and the ending key frames.

**Text or letters**
- None in the picture. The newspaper uses grey column bars, and the flags and scoreboard are colour stripes only.
- The only text is the captions. The caption "1963" uses Latin digits, which matches the script.

**Foam in 1963**
- None. Every 1963 landing is in sawdust.
- The blue slab under the newspaper (25.5-27.9 s) is a bleacher bench, not a mat. At phone size it could just be taken for a mat, so a wood or maroon colour would be safer (NICE).

**Frozen stretches**
- None. My every-frame scan found no 1 s window with a median frame-diff below 2.1.
- The calmest windows are 16.5-17.5 s (coach laugh, median 2.3) and 62-63.5 s (jumpers on the mat, median 2.2). Both have a moving camera and do not look frozen.
- The newspaper insert (25.4-27.9 s) is the most static-looking shot on the sheet: the same image with only a slow push.

**Measured numbers**
- Motion: median 6.3 and p90 13.8 meet the targets of >= 4 and >= 12 (film 2: 5.1 / 18.5).
- Loudness: -12.2 LUFS and -2.4 dBTP are in range.
- STT: every sentence is >= 0.975, except sentence 1. Its 0.91 is a Latin-letter false low, and the clean narration scores the same.
