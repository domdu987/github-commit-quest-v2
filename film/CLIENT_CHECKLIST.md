# The client's complaint checklist (the producer checks this on the VIDEO before sending anything)
Watch the film in motion: the full mp4, plus 6 fps strips over camera moves and walks.

- [ ] Nothing floats: props, flags, people and shadows all sit on the ground.
- [ ] No camera shake.
- [ ] Nothing is stuck to the camera: foreground props stay in place in the world.
- [ ] People who move actually walk (feet step), or they stand planted.
- [ ] No duplicate identical characters in the same frame, and near people are bigger than far ones.
- [ ] Contact shadows under every person.
- [ ] Characters are sharp, not blurry.
- [ ] No face fills the frame for more than about 1 s.
- [ ] No ghost double-exposure at the transitions.
