## Questions asked
- **Mockery with sound off, who / what / smugness:** who: yes (red-robed fat official with a purse and a gold chain, clearly the boss; Surrey trailing tired). Smugness: yes, in the face. What he points at: **no**. The arm points flat to the left and the Scots are specks on the hill behind him. The money motif (purse) is hidden by the barrel and never slapped.
- **Reference style:** mostly. The follow camera is good and the light is motivated (sun plus campfire). The near-ground set is thin.
- **CU1 at about 1 s:** the timing works. The content does not: no slap, and the push is too shallow.
- **Bridge and Scots across the river:** the bridge reads as a thin dark span with figures on it from about g 720 (in the left half of the track it is at the right edge or hidden behind the tripod). The Scots read only at 2x crop (saltires and dots at the foot of the crag). At phone size they are not findable.
- **Beautiful?** The far half of the frame is (crag, river, clouds). The lower half is flat sand and spiky grass, so the shot is pretty rather than beautiful.

## Rim light (known film-wide limitation)
The gold rim is barely visible at phone size on Cressingham and Surrey. It hurts little here: the black outlines and the red/blue/checker costumes against teal water already pop the characters off the background. This is not a reason for FIX-FIRST.

## Verdict: FIX-FIRST
This is a key shot and its punchline image (the purse slap) is missing, and the point target does not read. All the fixes are pose, camera and prop placement; no set rebuild is needed.

## Fixes (max 4)
1. **Make the slap real (CU1, g 756-765):** animate the free hand from shoulder height down onto the purse at g 758 (contact frame), with a 2-frame purse squash/bounce and a small coin glint or "jingle" pop. Hold the hand on the purse to g 770. Push 20-25 % tighter so the face is about 33 % of the frame height (still under the §G 35 % limit), framed head to belt so the purse stays in frame.
2. **Fix the point target (g 650-750):** raise the pointing arm 25-35 deg so the finger line runs up to the Scots at the foot of the crag. Then either frame the crag on that line, or bump the Scots on the hill (2-3x scale of the flag/figure group, a brighter blue/white saltire) so they are findable on the phone sheet. Bring the bridge into frame earlier (pan/offset 40-60 px or move the tripod) so both bridge and hill are present while he speaks "ข้ามสะพานนี่แหละ".
3. **Move the barrel off him (g ~690-718):** shift it about 0.8 m toward the camera and/or time it so it wipes through in 6-10 frames instead of parking over his body. Or change it to a non-red prop (a grey/blue crate or a stacked supply chest, ideally a money chest) so it never merges with the red robe and never covers the purse.
4. **Dress the near ground (g 642-700 especially):** break the bare sand with layered foreground props that the follow camera passes: tent pegs and ropes (the plan promises "through tent ropes"), spears in a rack, sacks, a strongbox, hoof prints and grass clumps. Or tilt the camera up 4-6 deg so the horizon sits lower and the sand takes under 30 % of the frame. Optional: a small painted sign at the tent ("ENGLISH CAMP" or "คลังหลวง", §I.4).
