## Verdict: FAIL

OK: high fly along the bridge then descent past the STIRLING BRIDGE sign is readable; two horses side by side fill the deck; camera stays above the rail, no rail crosses a face (rails cut horse barding only); no blur/DOF; no clipped sprites; no floating; no blood; no mouths moving (neither knight speaks); join with sD1 is a planned CUT.

## Issues
1. g~499-541 (n81-123, narration 16.52-17.97): the belly-suck beat is NOT readable. The rider bodies are hidden behind the horse barding and the front rail; only sweat drops and a flat squint show. No visible belly squeeze or knee-rail brush as the plan says. Fix: lower/tilt the camera slightly or lift the camera 0.3 m so the torso is above the rail, and give the near knight an explicit belly deflate (torso scale x0.8 with a visible gap, tabard going slack) with the horses nudging the rails, timed at g~524 (the "pff" cue).
2. g418-541 (all): the column pairs are mixed crimson and BLUE (blue-liveried knight on navy/white-striped barding in the lead pair; blue/yellow and white/blue pairs down the bridge). English must read crimson/gold; blue = Scots. At phone size the lead knight reads as a Scot riding with the English. Fix: recolour barding and tunic of the lead blue knight (and the blue pairs on the bridge) to English crimson/gold; keep Scots blue for Scots only.
3. g~500-541: the descent ends with the lead knights in the upper-middle and the lower 45 percent of the frame filled by the foreground rail and bare sand. Minor framing waste. Fix: tilt up about 5 deg or lower the end position so the knights sit at 40-60 percent of frame height.
4. Note (no fail): grey curved blade-shaped objects stand in the sky/horizon (g~480-541). Looks like smoke/spire; if it is meant as smoke, make it a soft plume.
