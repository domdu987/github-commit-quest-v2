CAMERA (production-lessons §10, film-2 rhythm, film/plan/shot_plan.md): every shot has a clear move (push/pull 6-10 %/s of framing + lateral slide ~40 px/s), quick push-ins on punch lines, whip/snap on reveals, no near-static hold > 1 s, foreground props 1-2 D from the lens in most shots (crates, spool, ladder rails, inkwells, bottles, rain-wet grass), laughing characters bounce (~0.06 D at ~3 Hz). Dissolves ~11 frames on location/time changes (marked DIS), hard cuts on beats and quotes (CUT). Target in-shot frame-diff median >= 4, p90 >= 12.
MOUTHS move only on the mocker's quotes: boss quote 18.32-23.20 ("ส่งข่าวทางลวดเนี่ยนะ! ... ข้าจะมานั่งเฝ้ามันเอง!", with a laugh just before) and false quote 38.46-40.80 ("เห็นมั้ย ลวดของเอ็ง ตายอยู่ใต้ดินแล้ว!"). Everyone else silent.

Locations: LINE (Maryland countryside beside the railway, 1843-44), STUDIO (Morse's painting room, 1825), CHAMBER (generic 1840s legislative chamber; front table with the telegraph).

## Bible addendum (skill style bible, commit 38b5918: references/bible/camera.md + scene-tricks.md; client overrides in SKILL.md win)
- **Framing change every 1.3-1.4 s**: 30 cuts/dissolves + about 22 in-shot SNAPS (13-25 frames, x1.3-x3, eased, peak 1.6-1.9x mean) = ~52 changes in 70 s. Every shot longer than 2.0 s gets one snap on its strongest word (shots 1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14 [3 snaps], 16, 18, 19, 20, 22, 23, 24, 25, 28, 29, 30). The rest drift (6-10 %/s push/pull, 20-60 px/s slide, eased, dying out before the cut). The camera never parks (max 6 frames < 0.25 px/frame).
- **Angle mix** (screen time): eye 35-40 %, establishing/vista 15-25 % (shots 2, 4, 8, 14, 25, 30 end), insert 15-25 % (1, 16, 22, 24, 26 tape), low 5-10 % (9, 10, 18 only), high 7-10 % (8, 11, 14). Low = camera 1.2 D above floor, pitch up 13.5-16.5 deg; eye = head centre +0-0.3 D, 1-3 deg down; high = 3.3-4.4 D up, 20-25 deg down. Roll 0.
- **Sizes** (22 mm): close head 300-350 px (2.2-2.6 D away), medium 170-230 px (3.4-4.6 D, default), wide 110-135 px (5.8-7.1 D). Head centre y 450-760; whole heads above y 880. Two-shots staged in depth (near head 1.36-1.46x the far one, 1.0-1.4 D nearer). Reacting faces >= 250 px (shots 6, 10, 26 mocker jaw-drop, 29 horror).
- **Joins**: smoothstep dissolves (11 f default, 9 f quick continuity, 13-17 f for place/time: shots 4, 8, 13, 19, 28; the LONGEST, ~17 f, into shot 30 the night ending). Hard cuts on punch lines (9, 10, 18, 29). Specials, each ONCE: one whip (into shot 2), one object wipe (the train crossing the lens into shot 25 or out of 3), one punch-in with focus lines (shot 10 on "เฝ้ามันเอง"), one white flash (none needed; optionally the spark in shot 7). Accents >= 6 frames before an outgoing dissolve.
- **Streak** only on the set on frames > 60 px/frame corner speed; characters always sharp. No DOF.
- **Character devices** (one to four places each): laugh tears + blush on the mocker (shots 9, 11), sweat drop (mocker shot 29), sleep Z (mocker shot 30, while yawning), brightness dip x0.75 for 0.4 s on "เงียบกริบ" (shot 15), eye glint only in the night close (30). Face swaps on the closed frame of a 3-frame blink. Never: impact stars, speech bubbles, question/exclamation marks.
- **Props change state on the beat word**: bottle capped (1, 23), letter opened (6), money bag dropped (12), furrow growing (14), register silent (15), empty bag (17), bottle lifted with glint (22), pulse parks in glass (24), tape spools on "ติ๊ด" (26), instrument slid across (29). One sound each.
- **Callbacks** (named framings): shot 23 repeats shot 1's camera and marks exactly (now with the reason); shot 30 repeats shot 26's chamber_table axis at NIGHT.
