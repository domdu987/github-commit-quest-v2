# FINAL_4 blind viewing

1. Hook: a mustached writer hates the tower "most in the world" yet is shown sitting down to eat on that same tower almost daily. Paradox. Picture: angry man under the tower (0-2s), then cut to him happily eating at a table on the tower (3-6s). Surprising and clear.

2. Maupassant mocks Eiffel ("ไอ้ช่างหมุด" crowd nickname). Promise: "if the tower is built, I will not look at it even once" (24-25s), later laughs that it will be scrap iron soon (36-40s). Artists petition says it is useless/ugly like a black chimney.

3. Mechanism: 20-year permit (33-36s); near the demolition date Eiffel runs antenna wires from the top to the ground to a hut where soldiers send wireless telegraph (43-52s). Taller means signal goes farther, so the tower becomes a giant antenna, military cannot do without it, city extends permit by 70 years (52-58s).

4. Ending: he eats daily in the tower restaurant, the only place in Paris where you cannot see the tower, so he "kept his promise" never to look at it. The mocker is forced to live on the thing he mocked, and his vow is kept by technicality. The final shot returns to the full tower.

5. Mismatches / unclear:
- 25-27s: "ไอ้ช่างหมุด" shown with crowd laughing plus a character with a hammer; reads okay but the hammer man at 27s looks like Eiffel only by context.
- 36.2-36.8s: Eiffel with sweat drop, then abrupt cut to the writer laughing in a cafe; the laughing happens in a cafe with other men, fine.
- 33.2-36s: permit scene is clear, but the official with sash is unidentified (mayor?).
- 40.8-43s: "twenty years pass" shown only by a dusk scene with a soldier and Eiffel-like man; time passage not obvious, no visible date.
- 44-48s: wires look like thin white lines; hard to tell they are antenna wires until the telegraph operator at 46.8s.
- 48-52s: glowing night city with white circles - signal spreading is abstract but readable.
- 59.8-62s: restaurant scene fine, but at 62-65s the view out of the window shows the city and no tower, which supports the joke; however the viewer is never explicitly shown "tower not visible" - it is inferred.
- 66-67.8s: final shots show the full tower at the end; fine as a punchline, though the 66.2s restaurant-sign close-up is slightly disorienting.
- Frame 0.2-2.8s: writer is under the tower with no visible mocking yet; hook subtitles ok.
