# V2 final review (independent, 1 fps sheets only)

Source: scratchpad/final2/sheet_0..3.jpg plus full-size f/t_NNN.png. Seconds are the sheet labels.
I can't judge camera shake from 1 fps stills, so this review doesn't cover it.

## Problems found

| Second | Where in frame | What |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | whole frame | **Ghost overlay.** A cross-dissolve leaves the tower-construction shot see-through over the café group. Characters, chairs and girders all show through each other, and a semi-transparent face sits next to the caption (lower right). This is the "ghost glass" look again. Use a hard cut or a much shorter dissolve. |
| 15 | top half | Camera too close. Three heads (red beret, top hat, woman) are cropped at the top edge, and the faces take up most of the upper frame. A long cream-coloured pole runs diagonally from the painter toward the table, and the hand holding it isn't clearly visible. |
| 16 | top edge | Camera too close. Both men's heads are cut off at the top edge, so only mustaches and chins show. |
| 16 | centre-right, over the petition | A quill pen with two orange balls hangs diagonally in mid-air over the paper, with no hand and no contact. It reads as floating. |
| 15-17 | right of the table | Quills stand straight up out of the inkpots. This is acceptable, but at 17s the quill at centre-right looks like it is balancing on its tip. |
| 17 | top half / bottom right | Camera too close: two angry faces fill the top half and the woman's head is cut by the right edge. A large coffee cup is cut by the bottom-right corner in the foreground. |
| 18 | left edge | A man's face is sliced in half by the left frame edge, and a big tablecloth and cup fill the bottom-left foreground. |
| 18-19 | right-middle, by the window | A thin green rod or stick floats in the air at window height with no visible support. |
| 19 | left edge + bottom centre | A partial figure (woman's hair and purple dress) is cut by the left edge, and a chair back intrudes into the bottom centre of the foreground. |
| 30 | centre | Workers on the scaffold platform have no visible contact shadows; their feet are hidden behind the rail. White blobs (sweat or debris effect) float around them. |
| 33-35 | bottom-left corner | **Giant foreground head.** The back of a brown-haired head and a black top hat in the extreme foreground take up roughly a quarter of the frame's bottom-left, cut by the frame edge. This is exactly the "giant head blocking the foreground" complaint. |
| 36 | whole frame | **Face fills the frame.** The laughing man's head is about 55% of the frame width, with his body cut at the bottom. The neighbours (red beret on the left, white-haired man on the right) are cut by the side edges. |
| 37-38 | left, mid-height (around y=45%) | A grey flat slab or tray sticks out from the left edge next to the red-beret man. Nobody holds it, so it reads as a floating object. |
| 37-40 | floor | The three standing men have very weak or no contact shadows on the checkered floor. Their legs look loose and spidery, as if hovering. This is minor but visible. |
| 40 | whole frame | Face too close again: the head is about 50% of the frame width, and the side characters are cut by the edges. |
| 63 | centre | Close-up of the seated diner: the face is about 45% of the frame width. This is borderline. He is seated at the table, so it is otherwise fine. |

## Minor / probably fine
- 0-1s: a thin pink-white horizontal streak at mid-left next to the man (bunting line or a laser-like artefact).
- 7-8s: two small white loop shapes float at the right post of the tent.
- 25-26s: a loose orange beam is cut by the bottom-right frame edge with no visible ground contact.
- 41-43s: thick brown wooden discs lie on the ground. They have no shadow, but they read as lying flat.
- 58s: the RESTAURANT sign is cut by a beam and the frame edge (acceptable).

## Fixed versus previous complaints
- People on the open ground now have contact shadows: 0, 9-10, 27-29, 33-35 (the main pair), 41-43 and 52-57s.
- The hanging loads at 12 and 25-29s hang on visible cables.
- The radio rings at 49-50s are fine.
- The seated diner at 3-5 and 60-64s is fine.

## Verdict
**No.** The film is not yet free of the client's complaints. It still has:
- a ghost dissolve at 13s
- a giant foreground head at 33-35s, plus the dark back of a head filling the foreground at 65s
- faces filling or cut by the frame at 15-17, 36 and 40s
- a floating quill at 16s and a floating slab at 37-38s
