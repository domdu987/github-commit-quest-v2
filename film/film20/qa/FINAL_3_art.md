# FINAL gate, reviewer 3: QA-art (opus), film 20 (film20_rc2.mp4, 68.2 s)

**Verdict: PASS with notes**

What I looked at: sheet_2fps.jpg, all 12 phone pages (phone_00..11, every 0.5 s tile), full-res fr2 frames at the questionable times (16.8, 25.2, 29.2, 31.2, 49.2, 51.2 s), side_by_side_film2.jpg, ref_shorts R3 and R5 sheets, and narration_timed.txt.
Times are the phone-sheet tile labels.

## 1. Defects (full-res 2 fps + phone)
No blocking defects. Characters stand on surfaces with contact shadows throughout: plaza 0.2-2.2, site 7.2-10.2, café 13.8-24.8, beams 28.8-30.2, Exposition 33.8-36.2, night plaza 41.2-43.2 and 52.8-54.8, restaurant 59.8-65.8.
Nothing floats. Faces read on the phone, and captions sit on chests or below chins, never across eyes or mouths. Takes join by travel or dissolve with no colour breaks except the one in the notes.

Non-blocking notes:
- 15.8-17.8 (petition insert): the artists' heads are cropped by the top edge, and disembodied pen arms cross the frame (16.8). It reads as a tabletop insert, so I accept it. A slightly higher framing would be better.
- 19.8-23.8 (vow): Maupassant holds the same window framing and pose for about 4 s, with only face changes. This is borderline for §I.5. The same window angle comes back at 36.8-40.2.
- 25.2 and 33.2, 55.2, 57.8, 61.8: dissolve frames with large ghost faces. They are short and acceptable.
- 28.8-29.8: the carried beam runs at neck height behind the workers' heads. It reads as carrying and is not a pole through a face.
- 51.2: the tower's lower legs render bleached white for about 0.5 s during the antenna flyover.
- 59.8-61.8 interior is dim warm brown, then 62.2-65.2 is a bright daylight terrace, then 65.8 is dim again. Lighting jumps inside one location, but each shot is clear.
- 55.8-57.2 reuses the 33.8-36.2 Exposition 1889 set for the 70-year extension, which happened about 1909. The banner date is anachronistic, but the "same official, now smiling" reversal reads well.

## 2. §G.4: "Is it as vivid, wide and grounded as film 2?" YES
- **Vivid:** deep blue skies, a saturated orange tower, a red and blue-checker café, and a purple-blue night with warm bulbs. This is at least film 2's punch, with no haze.
- **Wide:** more than half the tiles are wide or medium-wide: 6.2 bird's-eye site, 11.2-13.2, 26.2-27.2, 31.8-32.8 tower rising, 41.2-52.2, 58.2-59.2, 66.8-67.8.
- **Grounded:** feet and shadows are on the ground in every character shot. The extreme close-ups (24.8, 40.2) are 1 s punch-ins.

## 3. §I.7: "Would a viewer believe this is from the same channel?" (vs R3 office, R5 storm oil) YES
Same family:
- round-faced flat puppets with expressive brows and moustaches
- lit, saturated 3D sets with warm practical lights against cool skies (chandeliers, tower bulbs, the telegraph hut)
- bold blue plus a single accent colour (orange tower)
- centred white Thai captions
- in-world signs: PROTESTATION, EXPOSITION UNIVERSELLE 1889, RESTAURANT

It is weaker than the references in two ways: thinner ink outlines on set geometry, and plainer low-poly city blocks in the night flyover (48.8-51.2). Neither breaks the match.
