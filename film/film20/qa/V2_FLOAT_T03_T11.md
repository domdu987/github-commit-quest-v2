# V2 float hunt: T03 and T11

Independent review of the 1 fps contact sheets, with full-size frames checked where a contact-sheet cell looked doubtful. Frame positions are given for the 720x1280 frame.

## T03 (5.9-13.5 s), construction site, engineer, crane, riveters

1. **7.7-8.1 s (g_0231-g_0243), left edge, lower middle (x 0-80, y 900-1050).** A pale lilac, faceted, semi-transparent shape slides out of frame at the left edge. Nothing in the scene explains it and it has no ground contact. It looks like a ghost overlay or a foreground object clipped by the frame. Only a stray diagonal shadow stays behind at 8.1 s.
2. **8.9-10.9 s (g_0267, g_0297, g_0327), upper left under the tower truss.** Tan wooden posts hang down from the iron truss and end in mid-air above the ground. Their tips are pointed or dark, and there is a visible gap and no contact shadow. This is clearest at 10.9 s: the post at x 70-160 ends at y about 530, with ground and sky visible beneath it. These are floating posts or props.
3. **7.9-8.9 s (g_0237, g_0267), above the tent roof (x about 500 at 7.9 s, x about 580 at 8.9 s).** A thin vertical rod comes down from the truss and stops on or just above the tent roof. It reads as a stray pole or cable with nothing attached. This is minor, but it looks like an unexplained stick.
4. **About 13.4-13.5 s (g_0405), bottom left (x 0-240, y 950-1280).** Two riveters in the foreground are oversized and cut off at the waist by the bottom of the frame. They are a giant-heads foreground cut, and nothing shows what they stand on.
5. **About 13.5 s (g_0405), centre right, upper pair of riveters (x 430-560, y 665-860).** Their feet are hidden and no deck is visible under them. The lower pair has a clear deck at y about 1110, but the upper pair seems to sit against the truss with no visible footing. This one is uncertain: a platform may be hidden behind the lower pair's heads.

Checked and OK: the engineer at the table (6.9-9.9 s) has feet on the ground and a contact shadow. The worker under the tent is grounded. The barrels, table, tent and shovel are grounded. At 11.9-12.9 s, the box on the crane jib and the hanger at the right edge hang from a visible rigid rod and jib, so they are fine.

## T11 (25-33 s), crowd, beam carriers, high deck, finished tower

1. **27.1-29.1 s (g_0813-g_0873), mid frame (x 30-445, y 705-740).** The long dark beam the two workers "carry" is drawn behind both workers' heads, at cap height. It does not rest on their shoulders and they are not holding it. It reads as a beam floating behind them rather than being carried.

Checked and OK: the crowd at 25.1-26.1 s stands on the ground with faint contact shadows, and the bowler-hat man is set further back. The crane load at the jib tip hangs from a visible rod. The timber stack sits on sleepers. The deck workers at 30.1-31.1 s stand on the deck with small shadows under their feet; the white puffs and dashes are hammer FX, and the orange plate in the bucket is held. The wide shots of the finished tower at 32.1-33.1 s have nothing floating that can be seen at this scale. The blue roof cut at the bottom of the frame at 33.1 s is a normal foreground framing.
