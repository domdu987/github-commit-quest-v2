## Locations (Paris 1887-1909), all in ONE new set module set3d20
- SITE  — Champ de Mars building site 1887-88: the tower UNDER CONSTRUCTION (4 curved lattice legs on stone piers, timber scaffolding towers under the legs, creeper cranes, then the first platform, then the second), stacks of red-lead painted iron girders, rivet forges glowing orange (emitters), wooden workers' sheds, carts; background: the Seine, the Trocadéro palace (two towers), cream Haussmann buildings with blue-grey zinc roofs. States: stage 0 (legs to ~30 m), 1 (first platform), 2 (second platform), 3 (top / finished).
- CAFE  — a Paris café / salon 1887: crimson banquettes, gilt mirrors (no readable reflections needed), marble tables, gas lamps (emitters), newspapers on sticks, bentwood chairs; a tall window shows the tower stub on the skyline. The protest letter is signed here (a sheet with many scribbled signatures, no readable text).
- TOWER — the finished tower 1889 / 1909 on the Champ de Mars: full silhouette, warm red-brown "Eiffel red" paint, gas-lit at dusk; Exposition pavilions, crowds, flags (no text). 1909 state: a long antenna wire runs from the summit down to the ground near a small wooden military radio hut with a spark gap that flashes blue-white.
- RESTO — the 1889 restaurant pavilion on the tower's first floor: warm dining room with white tablecloths, gilt, gas lamps, big windows showing all of Paris (roofs, Seine, Trocadéro) but NO tower (we are inside it); the tower's own riveted iron lattice girders frame the room and pass outside the windows. Ending: Maupassant eats happily, back to the view.

## Look (client rules)
Sharp picture, NO depth-of-field. Wide dense lit 3D world, warm hard sun + blue-violet shade (hue 240-260), warm practicals, pleasing period colours (no harsh black/white). Rich detail: dense props and life. No text signs anywhere (newspapers greeked). Heads above y 880. Tower must read as the Eiffel Tower at phone size: curved splayed legs, big arches between legs at first level, two platforms, tapering spire, lattice (not solid).
