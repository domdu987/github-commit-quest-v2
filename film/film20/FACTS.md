## Anecdote (dramatised, framed "เล่ากันว่า" in narration and marked in the caption)
- Maupassant (a signer) often ate at the tower's restaurant because it was the only place in Paris from which he could not see the tower. Popular anecdote; JSTOR Daily gives it as "according to" Roland Barthes ("The Eiffel Tower" essay); travel/press sources call it legend. Not documented in Maupassant's own writing that we could verify.

## Invented (comic, does not contradict checkable facts)
- Maupassant's spoken promise "ข้าจะไม่ยอมมองมันสักครั้ง" — invented quote (series convention). The protest itself complains Parisians will have to look at it for twenty years, so the spirit matches.
- His second jeer about scrap iron at the 20-year permit — invented.
- The nickname "ไอ้ช่างหมุด" for Eiffel — invented.
- Timing: the anecdote belongs to 1889-1893 (Maupassant died 1893), before the 1909 rescue; the ending says "ตั้งแต่หอเปิด", so it does not claim he was alive in 1909.

## Deliberately NOT claimed
- Not an exact signer count (Wikisource list = 47 + "etc."): narration says "สี่สิบกว่าคน".
- Not that the tower intercepted WWI messages (true but unneeded; avoids extra claims).
- Not the 300-workers number (sources vary 150-300).
