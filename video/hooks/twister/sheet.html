แหล่ง (อ่านจริง ยกคำตรง):
• NOAA Storm Prediction Center, Tornado FAQ — spc.noaa.gov/faq/tornado/ : “Absolutely not! Stopping under a bridge to take shelter from a tornado is a very dangerous idea…” · “Winds from some directions can accelerate and get stronger when forced into tight spaces (Bernoulli effect).” · “Deadly flying debris can still be blasted into the spaces between bridge and grade, and impaled in any people hiding there.” · “Overpasses offer no extra protection and have proved to be deadly in tornadoes!” · ที่หลบ: “Go directly to an enclosed, windowless area in the center of the building -- away from glass and on the lowest floor possible.”
• NWS, Tornado Safety — weather.gov/safety/tornado-during : “Seek shelter inside a sturdy building immediately if a tornado is approaching.”
• NWS Norman (OK), 3 May 1999 overpass lessons : “Seeking shelter under a highway overpass is to become a stationary target for flying debris… Safety in such a location is merely an illusion.”
