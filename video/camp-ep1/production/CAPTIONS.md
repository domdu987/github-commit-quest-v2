## แหล่ง (ข้อความตรงตัว ตรวจ 24 ต.ค. 2026)

**NHS "Snake bites"** https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/snake-bites/
- "keep the part of your body that was bitten as still as you can"
- "try to remember the colour and pattern of the snake to tell the doctor or take a photo of it, from a distance and if safe to do so"
- "do not go near the snake, or try to catch or kill it"
- "do not try to suck or cut the venom (toxin) out of the bite"
- "do not tie anything tightly round the part of the body where the bite is"
- "do not take aspirin or ibuprofen, as they can make bleeding worse"
- ในคลิป EP.3 พูดว่า "แอสไพรินทำให้พิษงูแรงขึ้น" แหล่งเขียนว่า "ทำให้เลือดออกมากขึ้น" (งูเขียวหางไหม้มีพิษต่อระบบเลือด) คอมเมนต์ปักหมุดใช้คำตามแหล่ง

**USDA Forest Service "If You Get Lost"** https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/if-you-get-lost
- "As soon as you realize you may be lost: stop, stay calm, stay put. Panic is your greatest enemy."
- "If it’s nightfall, you are injured or you are near exhaustion, stay in place."

**กรมควบคุมโรค (โปสเตอร์ Drowning Victims: Rescue and First Aid)** ดู `video/phrai/ARC.md`
- "Dial 1669 or call a medical care unit nearby as soon as possible."
