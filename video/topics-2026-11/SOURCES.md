สัญลักษณ์:
- ✔ = ตรวจซ้ำกับหน้าเว็บจริงแล้ว (curl + ค้นข้อความตรงตัวในเนื้อหน้า, 2 พ.ย. 2569)

## 1. ลอยกระทงท่าน้ำแน่น (ฝูงชนอัดแน่น)

### วิธีเอาตัวรอดในฝูงชน
- ✔ "If it's too late to flee, the most important thing to do is retain your balance and stay upright... Should you fall, the weight of other bodies will pin you to the ground before you have a chance to right yourself. So stay on your feet."
  - https://theconversation.com/ten-tips-for-surviving-a-crowd-crush-112169
  - The Conversation · Mehdi Moussaïd (Max Planck Institute for Human Development) · 27 ก.พ. 2019, ปรับปรุง 31 ต.ค. 2022
- ✔ "Should the pressure become intense, fold your arms up in front of you like a boxer. In that position you can protect your ribcage and keep a few centimetres of space around your ribs and lungs so you can breathe."
  - หน้าเดียวกัน (ท่ายกแขนกันอก)
- ○ "When pushed, our natural reflex can be to resist the pressure and push back. In a crowd crush, however, resisting will be a waste of precious energy. Instead, let yourself be carried by the flow while always retaining your balance."
  - หน้าเดียวกัน
- ✔ "If you do want to try to work your way free, he says, you can try moving forward and diagonally toward the crowd's edges—but he notes this is only likely to work in limited, lower-density situations."
  - https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/what-to-do-if-youre-trapped-in-a-surging-crowd/
  - Scientific American · 3 พ.ย. 2022 · อ้าง Ali Asgary
  - ข้อควรระวัง: ทำได้เฉพาะตอนคนยังไม่แน่นมาก
- ○ "For example, don't try to get down to pick up anything. This happens often, especially in moving crowds. As soon as you stop, it not only harms yourself, it is creating this crowd domino effect"
  - หน้าเดียวกัน
  - ตรวจตรงตัวไม่เจอ: อาจเป็นเพราะเครื่องหมาย ' แบบโค้ง หรือ paywall

He says to keep a firm base and if you drop something don't try to pick it up. You're more likely to get pushed down and you won't be able to get back to your feet."
  - https://www.wkyt.com/2022/11/01/uk-police-chief-stresses-situational-awareness-people-attending-events
  - WKYT · 1 พ.ย. 2022 · หัวหน้าตำรวจ University of Kentucky (ไม่ใช่ตำรวจอังกฤษ)

### ทำไมคนตายทั้งที่ยังยืน (compressive / restrictive asphyxia)
- ✔ "People die standing up and those who fall die because the bodies on top of them exert such pressure that breathing becomes impossible."
  - https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/how-and-why-do-crowd-surges-turn-deadly
  - AP ผ่าน PBS NewsHour · 29 ต.ค. 2022
- ○ G. Keith Still: "It takes 30 seconds before you lose consciousness, and around about six minutes, you're into compressive or restrictive asphyxia. That's a generally the attributed cause of death - not crushing, but suffocation."
  - https://www.npr.org/2021/11/10/1054428087/after-astroworld-a-crowd-scientist-explains-the-deadly-dynamics-of-crowd-surges
  - NPR · 10 พ.ย. 2021
- ○ "Oxygen is your most precious resource. The vast majority of deaths in stampedes are caused by asphyxiation."
  - The Conversation (หน้าเดียวกับข้างบน)
- ○ "Traumatic asphyxia is a major cause of death in fatal crowd disasters"
  - https://www.nms.ac.jp/sh/jnms/2019/086060310.html
  - Motomura et al., J Nippon Med Sch 2019; 86: 310-321
- ○ "The majority of the victims demonstrated the classic findings of asphyxia by compression - petechial hemorrhages in the absence of associated fatal internal traumatic injuries."
  - https://www.aafs.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/AAFS-2004-G44.pdf
  - American Academy of Forensic Sciences · 2004 · เหตุบันไดไนต์คลับ E2 ชิคาโก
