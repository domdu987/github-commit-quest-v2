## Director notes on the first blocky proofs (turnaround.png / faces.png, 13:22-13:27) — must be fixed
- FACE TOO SMALL: on the sheet the eyes+brows span about 60-65 % of the head width and sit at about 45 % of
  head height; the mouth is wide. Ours span about 35 % and look like a small sticker in the middle. Scale up.
- BROWS: thick, dark, sharply angled slabs (thickness about 6-7 % of head width); ours are thin lines.
- EXPRESSIONS read wrong: "angry" is a smiling open mouth; sheet angry = brows down hard, open shouting
  trapezoid mouth with teeth/tongue. Match each sheet expression mouth shape (determined = flat short line,
  worried = brows up in the middle, pain = gritted teeth with one eye squeezed).
- Kong STUBBLE covers jaw and chin as a dense dot field; ours is a few freckles on the cheeks.
- HAIR: sheet = big chunky clumped locks with rounded tips, glossy, a fringe falling over the forehead;
  ours = a porcupine of thin needles. Build fewer, thicker, curved clumps (Kong ~25-35, zombie wet locks hanging).
- ZOMBIE FACE: eyes much bigger glowing white ovals with angry brow ridges; mouth big, open, dark inside,
  with uneven yellow teeth top and bottom; blood running from the mouth and forehead. Ours reads goofy.
- HANDS: rounded C-fists like the sheet; ours are flat blocks with a square hole.
- BODY/CLOTHES: arms are square tubes; sheet sleeves are soft rounded blocks with cuffs and folds. Kong's
  harness = shoulder straps + one diagonal strap; belt with 4-5 big pouches and a buckle; cargo pockets on the
  thighs; boots with chunky tread soles and laces. Zombie shirt needs torn jagged hems and sleeve ends,
  collar, pocket, rolled sleeves; trousers torn with holes showing grey skin; blood as splatter and drips, not
  round blobs.
- MATERIAL: fabric should show weave and dirt gradients; orange currently flat with brown blotches.
