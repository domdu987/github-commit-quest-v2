## Screen direction (holds for the whole episode)

- **Safety is screen right, the threat comes from screen left.** The direction of travel on the expressway, the lorry, the stair, the hospital and the building site are all to the right. The crash zone, the horde and the last runners are to the left.
- Profile and tracking shots of the road and the truck are taken from the median side, so the left barrier and the stair gate are in the background.
- Three moves go right to left on purpose: the driver thrown at the alarm car (S27-S30), Kong running back to the sedan (S69), and the flare (S105-S106).
- Dialogue pairs keep one line each: Kong right / junior left in SCENE 02; in the cab the camera stays ahead of the windscreen or on the driver's side; Praew left / Kong right under the expressway; at the gate outside = left, inside = right.
- S158-S159: Kong faces the lens with Tos on his right and the gap on his left, so the last eye movement reads as a choice between two screen sides.

## Rules every shot keeps

- Puppets stay upright and camera-facing. Nobody lies on the ground: the junior sits against the wheel, the knock-down of the screenplay is a stagger against the truck, Tos in the tipped car is a card rolled 80 degrees seen through the windscreen frame.
- Every spoken line plays on a close shot of its speaker with lip sync (or on Kong listening, for the phone), because one voice speaks for everyone.
- Drawings on twos, camera and lip sync on ones; walks and runs use `gait8` with planted feet; every still pose has breath, blink, rain, beacon or prop motion, and no shot is a held frame with a camera move.
- A figure whose feet are in frame never runs toward or away from the lens.
- Levels: RAIN 0.2, SPLASH 0.25, HAZE 0.3, TILT 0, DOF 0.25, BLOOM 0.55. Under the expressway and in the building site RAIN is 0 on the actors' plane.
- No status icons. Captions as in the hook.
- Resumable: one folder per shot, a frame that exists and verifies is skipped; within a scene B and C shots render first, A shots last.
