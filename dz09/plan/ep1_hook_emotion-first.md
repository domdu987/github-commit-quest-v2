# Shot plan: EP.1 "ทางด่วนนรก" opening hook, Director B (emotion-first)

Source: `story/MASTER_SCREENPLAY_V5_EP1_excerpt.md`, Scene 01 complete plus the first beat of Scene 02 ("คนขับพุ่งเข้ากัดเจ้าหน้าที่กู้ภัย. เสียงร้องท่ามกลางสายฝน"). Look: `spec/LOOK_SPEC.md` (flat unlit puppet cards in a lit Cycles world, one hard key, blue-violet shade, 22 mm lens, roll 0), `references/look.md`, `v3/README.md`, `v2/puppet/API.md`, `v3/stage/API.md`.

Director B's thesis: the viewer has to feel Kong's broken promise before the horror arrives, so the horror lands on a man we already pity. The radio scream in the first 3 seconds is the promise that horror is coming. The middle 30 seconds are a phone call that is given room. The last 10 seconds turn the stillness of the call into the stillness of the driver, and then that stillness breaks.

## Shots

### Shot 01: "จอดำ" with a voice on the radio (the first-3-second hook)
- **Time:** 0.00-3.00 s, frames 0-71 (72 f, 3.00 s).
- **Set:** SET-A, micro-dressing: Kong's shoulder radio clipped to the wet orange vest, the strap and the reflective stripe at the edge of frame. Almost everything else is black.
- **Framing and angle:** extreme close insert, eye level, 22 mm at about 0.35 D from the radio. The radio grille fills the lower middle and the red TX LED sits at about (360, 560).
- **Camera move:** already moving on frame 0, a slow push-in at 8 % per second (sine in-out), plus breathing handheld at 2 px. On "เฮ้ย!" (2.35 s) a 4-frame jolt of 10 px down-right, as if Kong flinched.
- **Cast:** none on screen (Kong's vest only).
- **Action:** near-black frame, the screenplay's "จอดำ" kept as a mood instead of pure black so the feed has an image at frame 0. Rain streaks cross the frame, lit only by the LED's red. The LED pulses with the voice: on while the far rescuer speaks, flickering on "เดี๋ยว!", and dead on the squelch at 2.75 s. Drops run down the grille.
- **Dialogue / caption (pale blue-grey, radio):** "ศูนย์ครับ / คนเจ็บไม่ตอบสนอง..." (0.10-1.90) then "เดี๋ยว! เฮ้ย!" (2.00-2.70).
- **Sound:** squelch open at 0.00; heavy rain heard close (on the vest and plastic), filtered by the closeness; band-limited radio voice with crackle; on "เฮ้ย!" a scuffle and a distorted shout, then a hard squelch and dead carrier hiss (2.75). The rain swells to fill the gap.
- **Light:** no key. Only the LED (red emission, blooms) and a faint blue-violet ambient on the vest edge. Value under 0.15 for 85 % of the frame.
- **Transition out:** hard cut at 3.00 on the hiss cut-off. The rain roar of shot 2 slams in on the cut.
- **Sets up:** "คนเจ็บไม่ตอบสนอง... เดี๋ยว! เฮ้ย!" is the same pattern the viewer then watches happen in shots 16-19 (an unresponsive person, then the attack) and again in Scene 02 when the bitten rescuer goes still. The radio itself pays off as Kong's equipment in Scene 04.

### Shot 02: the gridlock
- **Time:** 3.00-6.25 s, frames 72-149 (78 f, 3.25 s).
- **Set:** SET-A, full deck: a multi-car pile-up in the middle lanes; behind it, gridlock out to the vanishing point (taillights as red emission dots that shrink into rain haze); Jersey barriers; dark light poles; an overhead gantry with a green expressway sign; the rescue pickup at right with its red light bar turning; the city skyline as a far card with scattered lit windows.
- **Framing and angle:** establishing, high angle: camera 9 D above the deck, pitched down 18 deg, 18 mm. Horizon at 0.36 H. Kong is a 40 px head beside the crashed sedan, left of centre at about x 300.
- **Camera move:** true 3D (tier T): an eased crane-down and push-in from 9 D to 7 D with a 0.4 D truck right (sine in-out, 2.8 s), settling for the last 12 frames.
- **Cast:** KONG (tiny): standing beside the wreck with the spreader in his right hand, head turning from the car to the queue of traffic (an eased yaw from 0.3 to -0.4 over 1 s). RESCUER (tiny): at the driver's door of the sedan, working his pry bar in short strokes (arm cycle of 0.6 s).
- **Action:** the scale of the night: one rescue unit against an endless jam. Red light sweeps across the wet asphalt and the car roofs once every 1.0 s. Rain falls in sheets that are visible against the dark sky.
- **Dialogue / caption:** none.
- **Sound:** a full-band rain roar (close plus distant layers); car horns far and near (two blasts, panned left then right); one siren very far to the left; the light bar's motor whine; the pry bar's metal screech from the rescuer (small, at about 4.6 s).
- **Light:** key is the pickup's white floodlight (hard, 1 deg, from camera-left high at a 40 deg elevation, colour `#dfe6ff`), aimed at the crash car so the actors cast whole-figure shadows on the wet deck. Red rotating spot from the light bar (animated, 1.0 s period). Blue-violet ambient. The puddles mirror the red and the taillights.
- **Transition out:** cut.
- **Sets up:** the rescue pickup and its light bar (Scene 03, the siren that draws the swarm); the scale of the jam ("ภัยพิบัติเกิดเร็วเกินตั้งตัว"); the rescuer already at the door, so "เปิดได้แล้ว" in shot 14 has a cause.

### Shot 03: Kong at the wreck

- **Set:** SET-A, beside the crashed sedan (the crumpled front at frame left, glass on the deck).
- **Framing and angle:** medium, low angle: camera 1.2 D above the deck, 4 D away, pitched 15 deg up, 22 mm. Head about 200 px, head centre at 0.45 H. The rain falls against the dark sky behind his head; the red bar glows out of focus behind his shoulder.
- **Camera move:** push-in at 7 % per second with a 30 px per second slide right, then settling. Breathing handheld at 3 px.
- **Cast:** KONG, yaw -0.3 (looking toward the car door off frame left), expression `determined`, rain-wet costume variant. Right hand holds the spreader down by his leg (`prop_r` spreader); left forearm wipes rain from his brow (one gesture, eased over 10 frames, timed to 7.2 s). Blink at 7.6 s. Crowbar visible strapped to his backpack over the right shoulder.
- **Action:** a working man, tired and focused. The red sweep crosses his face twice (animated sprite tint, see Light).
- **Dialogue / caption:** none.
- **Sound:** rain on his vest and on the car roof (close); the spreader's motor ticking as it cools; the rescuer's pry bar off screen left (two screeches); a distant horn.
- **Light:** the floodlight key from camera-left; the puppet is unlit by design, so the red pass is a per-frame sprite `tint` that rises toward `#ffb8b8` for 6 frames whenever the bar points at him; the set behind gets the real red light.
- **Transition out:** cut on his glance down at the tool.
- **Sets up:** the crowbar on his pack (Scene 02, he pushes the infected off with it; shot 20 here); his beard and RESCUE vest for continuity; the cordless spreader as his tool.

### Shot 04: battery, three bars
- **Time:** 8.75-10.75 s, frames 210-257 (48 f, 2.00 s).
- **Set:** SET-A micro: the spreader's status panel in Kong's gloved hand.
- **Framing and angle:** raking close insert, 22 mm at 0.5 D, 20 deg rake along the panel. Three green battery bars about 260 px wide in the upper middle of the frame (y 450 to 600), clear of the caption band.
- **Camera move:** slide left to right at 40 px per second with a 6 % push, on the panel's long axis (sine in-out).
- **Cast:** KONG's hand only (the puppet's ball hand and glove cuff, the sprite cropped). His thumb presses the status button at 9.1 s: the bars light from one to three in 3 frames each.
- **Action:** the tool is full and ready; nobody has used it in earnest yet.
- **Dialogue / caption:** none. The bars carry it.
- **Sound:** button click; a three-step electronic ready chirp in sync with the bars; rain drumming on the tool's plastic housing. At 10.55 s the first phone vibration starts (pre-lap of shot 5).
- **Light:** the bars are emission (green, kept small so they do not break the blue, grey and red palette); the floodlight rakes the housing; drops on the panel catch the red sweep.
- **Transition out:** cut on the second buzz.
- **Sets up:** "ไฟแบต 3 ขีดใน EP.1" from the bible: the battery as a countdown resource for later episodes.

### Shot 05: the phone buzzes
- **Time:** 10.75-13.25 s, frames 258-317 (60 f, 2.50 s).
- **Set:** SET-A, Kong beside the sedan; the rescuer works at the door behind him, small and out of focus at screen left.
- **Framing and angle:** medium close, eye level: camera at head height plus 0.2 D, 3 deg down, 22 mm, head about 280 px at 0.44 H. The rescuer's head is about 70 px, 6 D behind, at x 170.
- **Camera move:** slow pull-out from 1.1x to 1.0x (sine), breathing handheld at 3 px.
- **Cast:** KONG: the expression goes from `determined` to `neutral` behind a blink at 11.0 when he feels the buzz. Left hand pats his chest pocket (eased, 10 frames), draws the phone out and raises it into the frame (12.2 to 13.0, `prop_l` phone). Gaze down at it. At 13.05 he reads the name: a blink, then `worried` (the swap on the closed frame), sweat drop off (it is rain anyway). RESCUER (background): still prying the door, with small bursts of effort.
- **Action:** the private world breaking into the job.
- **Dialogue / caption:** none.
- **Sound:** the phone vibrates against the vest in 0.4 s pulses (a rattle in the low mids); rain; the pry bar behind; the far siren.
- **Light:** as shot 3. When the phone rises, its screen glow adds a cool white on his chin, done as a sprite tint gradient (a radial `#e6ecff` lift of 8 % on the lower head).
- **Transition out:** match cut on the phone's motion into shot 6 (same screen direction).
- **Sets up:** the phone lives in his chest pocket (Scene 04: "ก้องมองโทรศัพท์ ยังไม่มีสายจากลูก").

### Shot 06: "น้ำเหนือ, 4 สายที่ไม่ได้รับ" and the watch
- **Time:** 13.25-16.25 s, frames 318-389 (72 f, 3.00 s).
- **Set:** SET-A micro: the phone in Kong's left hand at chest height, the wet vest and the deck out of focus below; the wrist watch on the same wrist, in the lower right of the frame.
- **Framing and angle:** insert over the shoulder, high (camera 0.6 D above the phone, pitched 55 deg down, looking down onto the screen as Kong does), 22 mm. Screen about 380 px tall, centred at y 520; the watch face at (560, 760), above the caption band.
- **Camera move:** slow push-in at 8 % per second (sine), handheld at 2 px.
- **Cast:** KONG's hand only. At 14.0 his thumb wipes the wet screen once from left to right (eased, 10 frames): the drops smear and clear, and the text reads sharp.
- **Action:** the screen (a texture drawn from code): caller photo, a small drawing of NAMNUEA in her maroon hoodie, smiling, ponytail; the name "น้ำเหนือ"; a red badge "4 สายที่ไม่ได้รับ"; the phone still buzzing (the screen bounces 2 px per pulse). Rain drops land on the glass and run (animated drop cards on the screen plane). The watch reads 21:47; its second hand moves on every second, the only exact clock in the hook.
- **Dialogue / caption:** none (the text on the screen reads in place; no extra caption needed).
- **Sound:** buzz pulses, now louder and closer; drops ticking on the glass; rain bed slightly ducked (minus 4 dB) so the buzz owns the moment; the watch is silent.
- **Light:** the screen is emission (white-blue, value 0.85) and the brightest thing in the frame; the red sweep passes over the wet vest below.
- **Transition out:** cut.
- **Sets up:** the four missed calls and her photo (Scene 05: she calls, "ครั้งนี้พ่ออย่าผิดสัญญานะ"; Scene 04: he checks the phone again). The watch (Scene 02: "INSERT: นาฬิกาข้อมือ เวลาผ่านไป", the 60-second rule) is planted here so the Scene 02 insert pays off a known object.

### Shot 07: he answers
- **Time:** 16.25-19.00 s, frames 390-455 (66 f, 2.75 s).
- **Set:** SET-A, Kong beside the sedan; the deck and taillights out of focus behind.
- **Framing and angle:** close, eye level, 22 mm at 2.4 D: head about 320 px at 0.42 H, chest under the caption band.
- **Camera move:** push-in at 6 % per second, breathing handheld at 3 px. Settles as the call connects.
- **Cast:** KONG: `worried`, looking down at the phone (pitch +0.6). At 16.9 he shuts his eyes for 14 frames (`sad` brows behind the closed frame): the guilt beat. He opens them, swipes to accept (the thumb rises into the frame bottom at 17.6) and lifts the phone to his ear (eased, 12 frames, finishing at 18.4); head roll 8 deg toward the phone. Blink at 18.6.
- **Action:** one breath before facing her.
- **Dialogue / caption:** none.
- **Sound:** a last buzz cut short by the accept tone (17.65); rain; his exhale (a small breath, audible because the rain is ducked); the call connects with faint line noise.
- **Light:** the floodlight key from camera-left; red sweep tint on his face every 1.0 s; the phone glow on his cheek fades out as the phone goes to his ear.
- **Transition out:** dissolve, 6 frames (the only soft join in the first half: we are entering the call).
- **Sets up:** his habit of picking the job over the call, which the daughter names in shot 10.

### Shot 08: "พ่อ วันนี้พ่อสัญญาว่าจะมารับหนูไง"
- **Time:** 19.00-22.25 s, frames 456-533 (78 f, 3.25 s).
- **Set:** SET-A, the same position; behind him the red bar blurs into a soft disc.
- **Framing and angle:** close tightening to extreme close, eye level, 22 mm: head from 330 px to 410 px, centre at 0.40 H.
- **Camera move:** slow push-in at 7 % per second over the whole line (sine), handheld at 2 px. This is the slowest, most patient move in the film.
- **Cast:** KONG listening: phone at the ear (`arm_l` hand at the ear, phone prop), expression `neutral`, then `sad` behind a blink on "สัญญา" (20.6): the brows drop, pitch +1 (eyes down). The listener rule: under 0.04 D of travel, blinks only, mouth still. A small swallow (2-frame squash 0.03 D) at 21.8.
- **Action:** her words land on his face.
- **Dialogue / caption (pale blue-grey, phone voice, NAMNUEA):** "พ่อ / วันนี้พ่อสัญญา / ว่าจะมารับหนูไง" (19.15-21.70).
- **Sound:** her voice band-limited like a phone (300-3400 Hz) but intimate and dry; under it, faint TV or room tone from her end (it says she is waiting indoors without showing it); rain on his side, minus 6 dB; the red bar's motor whine hardly audible.
- **Light:** as shot 7. Water drips from his hair line (a drip effect drawn on the sprite, three drops over 3 s).
- **Transition out:** cut.
- **Sets up:** the promise. "สัญญา" is the word Scene 05 returns to ("ครั้งนี้พ่ออย่าผิดสัญญานะ").

### Shot 10: "ทุกครั้งเลย..." (the breath)
- **Time:** 24.75-27.75 s, frames 594-665 (72 f, 3.00 s).
- **Set:** SET-A, a wider view: Kong at the Jersey barrier in three-quarter profile, the jam below and behind him, the rain visible in sheets between him and the camera.
- **Framing and angle:** wide, high angle (the angle "for a character who has lost"): camera 4 D above the deck, 6.5 D away, pitched 12 deg down, 22 mm. Kong's head about 120 px at 0.55 H; he is small against the jam.
- **Camera move:** the quietest move in the film: a 6 % pull-out over the shot (sine), handheld reduced to 1 px. No snap. The camera gives him room.
- **Cast:** KONG: three-quarter, yaw -0.6, phone at the ear. On "ทุกครั้งเลย..." (24.95) he freezes. At 25.7 a blink into `sad` with the eyes closed (held 30 frames), pitch +1. The free hand with the spreader sinks 0.1 D (the tool suddenly heavy). At 27.2 the eyes open.
- **Action:** nothing happens, on purpose: the rain, the red sweep and a man who has no good answer.
- **Dialogue / caption (pale blue-grey, phone voice):** "ทุกครั้งเลย..." (24.95-25.90). Then no caption until the cut.
- **Sound:** her line; then 1.8 s of the phone line's thin hiss under the rain; the rain bed returns to full level (the world presses back in); one far siren rises and falls; no music. The production rule "ห้ามดนตรีบอกให้ร้องไห้" is kept: only a low, tonal rain-and-city drone continues under the scene.
- **Light:** the red sweep crosses Kong and then the barrier, twice; the floodlight key throws his whole-figure shadow onto the wet deck toward camera-right, lengthening the loneliness of the frame.
- **Transition out:** dissolve, 8 frames.
- **Sets up:** "ทุกครั้ง": the pattern Scene 05 asks him to break.

### Shot 12: silence, then she hangs up
- **Time:** 30.00-33.00 s, frames 720-791 (72 f, 3.00 s).
- **Set:** SET-A micro: the phone as Kong lowers it from his ear, held at chest height; the watch on his wrist.
- **Framing and angle:** insert, eye level to the screen, 22 mm at 0.45 D. Screen about 360 px tall, centred at y 540.
- **Camera move:** tilt down with the phone as it comes away from the ear (eased, the first 18 frames), then a slow push at 6 % per second; handheld at 2 px.
- **Cast:** KONG's hand only.
- **Action:** the call screen shows "น้ำเหนือ" and a running timer (00:41, 00:42, 00:43). The line stays open in silence for 1.2 s (she says nothing; the screenplay's "น้ำเหนือเงียบไป"). At 31.9 the screen changes to the call-ended state ("สิ้นสุดการโทร") and her photo returns. A rain drop lands on the photo at 32.3 and runs down across her face.
- **Dialogue / caption:** none.
- **Sound:** the open line: a thin hiss, one small breath from her side (unvoiced; the actor's breath recorded as part of the take), then the three-tone hang-up beep at 31.9. The rain stays low until the beep, then returns to full on the cut.

## Risks (honest)

1. **The pipeline runs at 30 fps; this plan is 24 fps.** `v3/shots`, the timing helpers, the blink lengths and the spec's measured numbers are all in 30 fps frames. Either convert every frame constant (blink = 1 frame each at 24 fps still reads; dissolve and snap lengths scale by 0.8) or render at 30 and conform to 24 with frame dropping, which judders the camera moves. Converting is cleaner and needs checking with `qa_motion.py`, whose thresholds also assume 30 fps.
2. **No night exterior exists yet.** Only the office (and a beach under construction) is built. SET-A is a new set with a car interior, about 30 vehicles, a light bar, rain and wet surfaces: the largest single build in this plan, and the riskiest for render cost (rain cards and reflective puddles raise the Cycles noise at 12 samples; expect to need more samples or a denoised plate for the wet deck).
3. **Colour band.** The hue targets in `references/look.md` come from daytime references (red 30 to 41, orange 22 to 38 percent of chroma). A night EP.1 frame will sit far outside them on purpose: much more blue, more dark pixels (likely 40 to 60 percent under value 0.25 instead of 8 to 15) and little orange apart from Kong's vest. `hue_report.py` will flag every shot; the brief (dark blue, grey, emergency red) has to win and be stated as the reason.
4. **Puppets are unlit cards.** The red sweep on faces, the phone glow on the chin and the hazard light on the driver all have to be faked with per-frame sprite `tint`. A global tint multiplies every fill evenly, so there is no directional light on a face; it may read flat next to a lit set. A gradient tint needs a small extension in the sprite page.
5. **New face drawings.** The infected face (a ring eye with no pupil, a grey fill, a lip stain) and the failed small smile timing are not in the 20 measured expressions. They must be added in an extension module; a reviewer may call the infected face too cartoonish for horror, since the puppet vocabulary is comic by origin.
6. **The head lift.** The puppet head is always a full disc with the features visible; `pitch` only moves features by 0.07 D per unit. "Head down, face hidden" depends on the steering wheel rim and the dashboard shadow occluding the card, plus a lean. If the occlusion fails, the face is visible before the reveal and the shot 17 payoff is lost.
7. **The bite and the grab.** Two overlapping cards in one plane speckle in Cycles; the stage uses a depth bias, but a head pushed "into" another figure's shoulder may need both figures drawn into one combined sprite for shot 19. The puppet has no back views, so the bite must stay in profile, and the driver's lunge through a door frame from inside a car is the hardest staging in the plan.
8. **Kong holding two objects and a phone at the ear.** The arm IK reaches 0.74 D; phone-at-ear with a held spreader and the reach to the crowbar handle over the shoulder (shot 20) may exceed reach or clip the head; probe with `probe.py` first. The phone, spreader, crowbar and Halligan bar are new prop types that the puppet does not have yet.
9. **Voices.** The plan needs four voice takes in Thai (radio voice, Namnuea, Kong, the rescuer and his scream). The pipeline's TTS route (fal.ai ElevenLabs) is a paid API, which this task forbids, so the voices must be recorded or sourced. A TTS scream will not work anyway. Until voices exist, the 2.25 to 3.25 s dialogue windows are estimates at a natural pace and must be re-timed to the real reads.
10. **The first 3 seconds are mostly dark.** Shot 1 honours the screenplay's "จอดำ" with a near-black frame and a red LED. On a phone in daylight, a dark frame plus a voice could still lose swipers; the hook rests on the radio audio ("เดี๋ยว! เฮ้ย!" at about 2.0-2.7 s) and on the captions. If testing shows drop-off, the fallback that keeps the screenplay is to brighten the rain streaks and let the red light bar's glow sweep through frame at 1.0 s, still without showing the location.
11. **Emotion-first pacing versus retention.** Shots 7 to 13 (about 19 s) contain no threat on screen. The radio scream, the rescuer prying the door in the background of shots 5, 9 and 13, the seat-belt chime and the rising handheld are there to keep the tension alive, but a retention test may still show a dip around 25 to 30 s (shot 10, the breath). The cut-down option is shot 10 to 2.25 s and shot 12 to 2.5 s, which saves 1.25 s without losing a line.
12. **Added wordless details.** The caller photo, the watch time 21:47, the seat-belt chime, the driver's hand twitch, the headlamp and the optional phone glow in shot 20 are not in the screenplay. None changes an event, but the phone glow in shot 20 could be read as a new call; it is marked optional and should be cut if a reviewer reads it that way.
13. **Render time.** Every shot is a real 3D shot in this plan (a moving camera through a rain set). At about 5 s per frame in the existing v3 pipeline, 1296 frames is about 1.8 hours of Cycles on 4 cores before retakes, more with rain and reflections. Inserts 1, 4, 6, 12 could drop to the plate tier (one still plus 2D moves plus animated overlays) to save time, without breaking the "no still image standing in for animation" rule, because the hand, the drops and the screens still animate.
